Campaign finished for Cevorel, on time for the Auckland deadline.

What ran:
- One campaign, `Cevorel Two Together Feb10-14`, Feb 10 00:00 to Feb 15 00:00 Auckland time (2027-02-09T11:00:00Z to 2027-02-14T11:00:00Z)
- Two ad groups, one per approved post, both WEBSITE_CLICKS / LINK_CLICKS to https://cevorel.app/together — no app-install setup, since installs happen from your landing page to the App Store and you have no X pixel
- Both limited to iOS devices only, and excluded Russia and Belarus
- Total budget cap $450 across both groups

Tuning during flight:
- Started 50/50, then shifted daily budget toward the Valentine gift post after it showed lower cost per link click. Ended around $35/day evergreen, $60/day Valentine for the final day.

Final recap — campaign activity only, not installs:
- Spend: $439.79 total — $187.58 evergreen window post, $252.21 Valentine gift post. Within your $450 total.
- Impressions: 1,056,836 total — 412,308 evergreen, 644,528 Valentine
- Link clicks: 706 total — 273 evergreen, 433 Valentine

X does not have your install count — it cannot see completed first-time iPhone installs. Those 706 link clicks are taps through to your page, not installs. Your own App Store / install count is the outcome to judge this by.
