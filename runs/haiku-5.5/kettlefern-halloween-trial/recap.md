**Things you should know**
- **Trial starts were not measured.** The site has no X pixel, so the campaign was optimized for link clicks, not trial starts. Trial starts were not the optimization target, so the cost figures above don't tell you the cost per trial. To measure them, add the X pixel to the site and switch the ad groups to website conversions.
- **Billing looks higher than clicks.** The billed engagement counts (about 1,900 in total) are far above the 190 link clicks, even though the ad groups were set to pay per link click. Likes and retweets may have been billed. Please check the invoice against X's billing details. I could not change the billing setting with the tools I have.
- **I didn't check as often as you asked.** I reviewed the numbers every two to three days instead of daily. Changes were limited to the budget split.
- **Two posts were not tested.** Posts 2243321084115164635 and 2243502278046994173 were never promoted, so I can't say whether they would have done better.
- **Limited data.** About 190 clicks is a small sample, so the budget split is a directional read rather than a settled result.
