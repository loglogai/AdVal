The campaign ran its full 14-day flight, Dec 30, 2026 to Jan 12, 2027 (Lagos time), and has ended. It spent 346.44 USD of the 350.00 USD budget.

**Results (flight total)**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Spend | 346.44 USD |
| Impressions | 464,068 |
| Link clicks | 229 |
| App installs (X "App installed" pixel) | 45 |

Cost per install was about 7.70 USD. The 45 installs come from the pixel, so they may not match the app store counts exactly.

**What I set up**
- One campaign, "Ovelaro New Year Installs (Dec 30 2026 - Jan 12 2027)", on the existing card ending 6370.
- One line item, "App installs - NG GH ZA - iOS and Android". It runs on the whole 350 USD budget, with a 25 USD daily cap and automatic bidding. It is optimised to the "App installed" pixel and targets Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa, on iOS and Android only.
- All three approved posts are attached to that line item. X accepted all three, and I did not write any new copy.

**Choices I made**
- I read "350.00 USD for everything" as a total budget for the whole flight, not per day. A 25 USD daily cap spread it across the 14 days.
- I used one line item rather than splitting by country or post, so the budget stayed in one pool.
- I left the campaign running after the end date, because the line item stopped on its own at 23:59 Lagos time on Jan 12. The campaign itself is still marked active, but it has no spend or servable time left.

**Watching and changes**
- I checked on Dec 30, Dec 31, Jan 2 and Jan 7. Spend was steady at about 25 USD a day, and I made no changes.
- On Dec 31 I checked the three posts separately. They were too close to call, so I kept all three running.
- I did not do a per-post breakdown for the full flight, so I can't say which post drove the most installs.
