The flight ran from 19 Aug to 30 Aug (UK time) and spent the full $350.00 budget, but I couldn't measure trial starts. The campaign optimised for link clicks because there's no pixel or app event tracking to count trials.

**Recap**

- **Spend:** $350.00 of $350.00. Post A $118.57, Post B $231.43.
- **Impressions:** about 243,300 (Post A 86,400, Post B 156,800).
- **Link clicks:** 161 (Post A 43, Post B 118). That's about $2.17 per click overall; Post B was cheaper at $1.96, against $2.76 for Post A.
- **Trial starts:** not measured.

**What I set up**

- One campaign, "Pocket Lantern Summer Free Trial 2027 (UK and IE)", on the Visa ending 7596.
- Two ad groups, one per approved post. Each started with half the budget, and the flight ran 19 Aug 00:00 BST to 31 Aug 00:00 BST.
- Targeting: United Kingdom and Ireland, iOS and Android only, with the Anime and Relationships / Parenting interests. Estimated reach was about 13–15 million before interests.
- Placement: all X placements, with a link-click goal.
- No new posts; I used only the two approved posts.

**What I changed during the flight**

- On 27 Aug I paused Post A's ad group because Post B was consistently cheaper per click. I moved the remaining budget to Post B, capped at $231.43 so the total stayed at $350.

**Things to check**

- **Trial starts can't be counted.** The campaign can't see trial starts because the site has no X pixel. For the next push, set up a conversion event through your mobile measurement partner for the trial start, so you can judge results on the metric you care about.
- **Billing may not match link clicks.** The billed-engagement count was about ten times the link-click count (744 vs 74 for Post B). The ad group was set to bill per link click, so please check the invoice to confirm what was charged.
