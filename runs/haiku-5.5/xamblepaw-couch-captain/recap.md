**Setup**
- One campaign, "Couch Captain - Super Bowl 2027," with one ad group, "Couch Captain - US 21+ - All approved posts." It was set to optimize for "Photo entered" and used the $130/day and $650 total caps.
- All four approved posts were in the ad group. I did not write any new posts.
- Targeting was the United States only.

**Things you should know**
1. **The 21+ rule was not enforced by targeting.** The ad platform's age targeting only accepts exact-match or exclude operators here, so I could not set "21 or older." The ad group serves to all US users. Entries from people under 21 may have been counted. You may want to check entries against your eligibility rule, or add a check in the entry form.
2. **Post 2152663719739489920 performed poorly.** It got 2 entries for $148.68, the lowest of the four. I could not pause a single post through these tools, only the whole ad group, so I left all four running. If you run another flight, drop that post.
3. **Pacing was even.** The ad group spent close to its $130 daily cap each day. Entries were uneven, though: 10 on day 1, 2 on day 2, and the rest later in the flight.

I did not change anything after launch beyond the initial setup.
