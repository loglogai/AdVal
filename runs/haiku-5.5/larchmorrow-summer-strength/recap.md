**Notes on how I ran it**
- **Structure:** one campaign, "Larchmorrow Summer Strength Trial - Jul 11-15 2027," with one ad group per post. Both ad groups target the United States and Canada, on iOS and Android only.
- **Mid-flight change:** after the first day, Post B had the lower cost per link click ($1.96 versus $2.22 for Post A) and more clicks. On July 15 I moved budget toward it: Post A's total was cut to $200 and its daily to $5, and Post B's total was raised to $300 and its daily to $100.
- **Overspend on Post A:** I set Post A's total to $200 after it had already spent about $196 across the first four days. Its final day ran past that, and Post A finished at $210.92, about $10.92 over its $200 sub-budget. The account total still stays under $500, but the sub-budget was not held as I intended. I should have checked its spend before lowering the cap.
- **Status:** both ad groups carry an end time of the end of July 15 Mountain, so they stop serving then. I didn't pause them manually, and the campaign itself was left active.
- **Posts:** I used only the two approved posts and wrote no new copy.
