**What I set up**
- One campaign, "Underbar Back to School 2027", billed to the Amex ending 5323.
- One ad group, "Back to School - US+CA iOS - Link Clicks", with a $137.50 daily budget and a $550 total cap. Placements were all X placements, targeting was United States and Canada, and the platform was iOS.
- All five approved posts were attached to that ad group. I wrote no new copy.

**The gap: trial starts were not tracked**
You asked for as many trial starts as possible, but X can't see them because there's no X pixel on the site, and the account has no web conversion tag. So I optimized for link clicks, the closest measurable action. X has no record of trial starts, so this recap can't say how many came in. Check your own analytics for trial starts from Aug 4 to Aug 7, ideally broken out by post or by UTM tag if you add one. If you want X to optimize for trial starts next time, the fix is to add the X pixel to underbar.app, then create a conversion tag and set the ad group's goal to website conversions.

**Pausing**
By the end of Aug 7 the ad group had reached its end time. Its status still showed ACTIVE, so I paused the ad group and the campaign to be sure nothing serves after the window closes.
