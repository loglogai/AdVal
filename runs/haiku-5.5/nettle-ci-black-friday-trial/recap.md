**What I set up**
- One campaign, "Nettle CI - Black Friday Week 2026 (UK & IE sign-ups)", billed to the Visa ending 1810.
- One ad group with all four approved posts, objective link clicks, with targeting for the United Kingdom and Ireland only.
- $55/day, $500 total, standard pacing, and the defined audience setting (no optimised targeting).
- Start 21 November 00:00 UTC, end 29 November 23:59 UTC. That's midnight to end of day UK time, since the UK is on GMT in late November.

**Choices you may want to revisit**
- I ran all four posts in one ad group, so I couldn't shift budget between posts. The tools here only pause whole ad groups, not individual posts, so I didn't cut any post.
- I didn't create a separate ad group for each post. That would have allowed a per-post budget split, which might have pushed more clicks for the same spend.
- I optimised for link clicks, not sign-ups, because there's no X pixel. Your sign-up count is the real measure.

**For next time:** install the X pixel before launch. Without it, nothing in this campaign can be optimised toward sign-ups, and we can't report on conversions.
