**What I did**
- Created one campaign with two US-only ad groups, both using the four approved posts and no new copy.
- Used link clicks to the season page as the goal, since that's the closest measurable action available. Clicks don't prove someone started an episode.
- On Oct 26, I moved budget toward the podcast-listeners group, which was clearly cheaper per click. I cut the horror group's total from $350 to $200 and raised the podcast group's from $350 to $500. The total stayed at $700.
- The campaign ended on its own at the scheduled end time, so nothing is still running.

**Things to know**
- The podcast-listeners interest audience performed better than the horror audience. If you run this again, start with more budget on that audience.
