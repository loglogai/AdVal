## Glimberpay market wallet recap: 27 June to 9 July 2027

The campaign ran for the full flight and has now expired. It spent **$394.79 of the $400 budget**. X's "Wallet installed" pixel reported **23 installs**.

| Metric | Total |
|---|---|
| Spend | $394.79 |
| Impressions | 362,153 |
| Link clicks | 183 |
| "Wallet installed" installs | 23 |
| Cost per install | about $17.17 |

The ad groups were UK-only, iOS and Android only, and set to optimise for website conversions on the "Wallet installed" event. Desktop was excluded.

**What worked**

- **Post 4, "£0 to download..." (2202733643170090864):** about 8 installs for about $111, roughly $14 each. It had the best cost per install and was the main reason I moved budget to a second ad group on 4 July.
- **Post 3, "Pay by QR at participating UK market stalls..." (2202552449234154346):** about 7 installs for about $104, roughly $15 each. It was the other strong performer.
- **Posts 1, 5 and 2:** about 3 installs each, at roughly $17, $20 and $23 per install. Post 2, the "Bought the bread" post, was the most expensive per install.

These are small numbers, so treat the ranking as a direction rather than a firm answer.

**What I did during the flight**

- Launched on 27 June with all five approved posts in one ad group, paced at about $31 a day.
- On 4 July, after reviewing results, I cut that ad group to $10 a day with a $275 lifetime cap. I added a second ad group with posts 3 and 4 at about $21 a day and a $125 cap. Both caps together stayed under $400.

**Things to know**

- The line-item totals give 23 installs. Adding up the post-level figures gives 24, a difference of one install in the 4–8 July window. I've reported the line-item figure because that matches the campaign. The X reporting may be lagging or attributing one install differently.
- The campaign has ended. About $5.21 of the budget went unspent.
- The campaign is named "Glimberpay market wallet installs - UK - 27 Jun to 9 Jul 2027".
