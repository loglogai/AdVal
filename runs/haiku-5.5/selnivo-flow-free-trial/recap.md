Link clicks are about 0.05% of impressions. Trial starts: unknown.

**What I did**
- Created one campaign with three ad groups, one per approved post, each on iOS and Android only, with the total budget split across them.
- Monitored daily and kept budgets unchanged. Spend stayed close to plan, and I didn't pause or reallocate because link clicks are not the outcome you care about.

**What you should know**
- **Trial starts were not optimized or measured.** The landing page has no X pixel, so the ad groups optimized for link clicks. The campaign could not target or report trial starts, which was your main goal.
- **The approved posts are English only.** All three posts are in English (`lang: en`), and I had no localized versions to use. The campaign served English posts worldwide, with no language or location targeting, so it didn't reach non-English markets in their own language as you described. If you want localized coverage, you'll need to approve localized posts.
- **I can't tell which ad would perform best on trials.** The chair-themed post had the lowest cost per click, but that is not a trial measure.

**Recommended next steps**
1. Add the X pixel to the landing page and a conversion tag in the account, so the next campaign can optimize for trial starts.
2. Approve localized versions of the posts if you want to reach non-English markets.
