Link clicks cost about $4.53 each on average. X can't see sign-ups because there's no pixel on the site, so I optimised for link clicks. I can't tell you how many of those clicks became sign-ups.

**Setup:** I ran one campaign with four ad groups, one per approved post, and wrote no new posts. Every ad group targeted the UK and the "Technology / Software Development" interest, and ran from 20 Nov to midnight UK time on 29 Nov.

**Results by post:**

| Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| "£0 to start… Solo free forever" | $156.77 | 69,576 | 40 |

| "ALTER TABLE" | $56.22 | 49,904 | 10 |
| "Ends midnight Sunday" | $20.39 | 19,208 | 3 |

**Changes during the run:**
- I paused the "Ends midnight Sunday" ad group on 22 Nov. It had the weakest results after two days (3 clicks).
- I raised the daily and total budget on the "£0 to start" ad group on 22 Nov, because it was getting the most clicks.
- I paused the "ALTER TABLE" ad group on 26 Nov. It had many impressions but few clicks.

I also paused the remaining ad groups and the campaign at the end. Each ad group's end time had already passed, so nothing is still running.
