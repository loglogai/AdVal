- **Excluded countries:** I did not exclude Iran, North Korea, Cuba or Syria, so the ads could have shown there. I set no location targeting at all. Any impressions or clicks from there can't have turned into streams, since the release isn't distributed in those countries. I can't tell you how many there were.
- **Bonus duet after the weekend:** Three of the five posts mention the bonus duet. They kept running on Monday, after "Fremantle, 2am" was due to come off the release. You asked for Monday to be covered, so I left them running. Some Monday clicks may have gone to a release without the duet.
- **Changes during the run:** I made none, and I paused nothing. Everything stayed active and I used no other posts. The early checks showed no obvious loser, so I didn't pause or shift budget between posts or ad groups. Per-post results are in the X dashboard if you want to compare them.
