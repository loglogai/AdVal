That works out to about $5.36 per link click. The two windows are separate because the stats tool caps each request at about 7 days. I added the totals myself.

**How it was set up**
- **Structure:** One campaign ("Black Friday 2026 - UK&IE sign-ups") with one ad group, which ended at 00:00 on 30 November. The ad group had a $55/day budget and a $500 total cap, with automatic bidding, optimised for link clicks.
- **Targeting:** I targeted the United Kingdom and Ireland only, plus the "Technology / Software Development" interest. I did not widen the audience beyond that.
- **Placements:** Timeline, search and profile.
- **Posts:** All four approved posts ran together. I wrote no new ones.

**What I saw**
- **Post performance:** At the 26 November check, the "Until Sunday 29 November" post and the first post (the one naming UK & Ireland teams) each had 20 link clicks. The "£0 for 60 days" post had 4 clicks from about 21,800 impressions, and the "rerun job" post had 10.
- **No changes after launch:** I didn't pause or reallocate anything. With clicks this low, the gaps between posts were too small to act on, and the stats don't show which post produced sign-ups.
- **No sign-up data:** X can't see sign-ups without the pixel, so I optimised for link clicks. The real test is your own sign-up count against these figures.
- **Check-ins:** I looked at performance on 23 and 26 November, then again after the campaign ended. I didn't check daily, so I can't say whether pacing varied day to day.

The campaign is no longer serving, and nothing else in the account is running.
