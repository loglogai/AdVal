The campaign has finished. It spent $426.85 of the $450.00 budget and got 363,827 impressions and 202 link clicks.

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Spend | $426.85 |
| Impressions | 363,827 |
| Link clicks | 202 (about $2.11 per click) |

**Setup**
- **Targeting:** Ireland and the UK only, since that's where the album is available.
- **Ad groups:** two ad groups at $25 a day each. One promoted the "Halloween sound" and launch-announcement posts. The other promoted the "Free" post and the descriptive post.
- **Posts:** I used four of the five approved posts. I left out "Our new album is out now. Hope you enjoy it!" because it says nothing about the album.
- **Schedule:** both ad groups ran from the launch until the end of 31 October, Dublin time.

**During the run**
- I checked on days 3 and 6.
- The four posts performed about the same, so I made no changes.
- Roughly $23 of budget went unspent because of the daily caps.

**Streams**
I can't report streams. The site has no X pixel, so these numbers only show people who clicked through to the landing page. Your streaming services' own dashboards will show how many of those clicks turned into plays.
