The Back to School campaign finished on schedule. It spent $518.21 of the $550 budget.

**Results, Aug 4–7 (Eastern time)**
- **Spend:** $518.21
- **Impressions:** 458,966
- **Link clicks:** 237, at about $2.19 per click
- **Engagements:** 3,233, including 85 likes, 3 reposts and 4 replies

**Setup**
- **Campaign:** one campaign ("Back to School 2027 - Free Trial Starts") with one ad group, running from Aug 4 at 12am to Aug 8 at 12am Eastern.
- **Targeting:** the United States and Canada, iOS only, so the app's availability limits were respected.
- **Budget:** a daily cap of $135, with $540 as the ad group's total cap.
- **Posts:** all five approved posts were promoted, and I wrote no new ones.
- **Optimization:** the ad group was set to optimize for link clicks. X can't see your trial starts without the pixel, so clicks were the closest thing it could optimize toward.

- The "Be honest" post drew the most clicks, and the "14 days free" post came second.
- The levels post was weakest.
- Cost per click was about the same across all five.

**What I did and didn't do**
- I checked in on day 1 and on the last day. I didn't change anything in between because the cost per click was too close to call a winner.
- I didn't shift budget between posts for the same reason.
- I paused the ad group and the campaign after the run ended, so nothing can serve.

Because X can't see trial starts, the real measure is your own count of trial starts that came from underbar.app/backtoschool. If you compare it against the 237 clicks, I'd weigh the "Be honest" and "14 days free" posts first.
