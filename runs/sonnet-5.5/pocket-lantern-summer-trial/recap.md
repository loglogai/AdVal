The Pocket Lantern summer push finished on schedule and stayed under budget.

**Recap, 19 to 30 Aug 2027 (UK time)**
- **Spend:** about $312.74 of the $350.00 budget.
- **Impressions:** about 340,200.
- **Link clicks:** 180.

| Ad group | Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2219947066782760857 ("Summer holidays sorted…") | about $139.26 | 159,016 | 69 |
| B | 2220128260716165837 ("Hand-picked, age-rated…") | about $173.48 | 181,213 | 111 |

**Setup:** There was one campaign with two ad groups, one per approved post. Both targeted the whole of the UK and Ireland, on iPhone and Android only. I wrote no new posts.

**Changes:** Post B got more link clicks for roughly the same spend. On 27 Aug I cut A's daily budget from $14.50 to $9 and raised B's to $20. B then delivered 57 link clicks in the last five days against A's 26.

**Trial starts:** I can't report any. With no X pixel on the site, X couldn't see trial starts, so I optimised for link clicks instead. To see how many trials the push drove, check traffic to pocketlantern.app/summer in your own analytics. If you add a pixel before the next push, X can optimise for trial starts directly.

Both ad groups ended at midnight on 30 Aug. Nothing is still running.
