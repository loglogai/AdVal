The campaign ran 19–30 July and is now finished. I have no email tool, so the recap is here.

| | Ad A ("long evenings") | Ad B ("stream free, no sign-up") | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | about $247.08 | about $100.95 | about $348.03 of your $350 |
| Impressions | about 257,600 | about 97,900 | about 355,400 |

- **Setup:** I set up one campaign with two ad groups, one per approved post. I didn't write any new posts. Both targeted the United Kingdom and Ireland, optimised for your "Release opened" pixel, with an even split of the budget. They ran from the start of 19 July to the end of 30 July, British Summer Time.
- **Mid-flight change:** After about a week, ad A was getting more streams per dollar than ad B, so I paused ad B on 25 July. I moved its remaining budget to ad A by raising ad A's daily and total caps.
- **Cost per stream:** The whole campaign came to roughly $3.08 per stream. The final weekly check was split across date ranges because the stats tool caps each query at about 7 days, so the totals above are approximate, to within a dollar or so.
