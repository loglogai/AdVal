The pixel counted 68 "Album stream" events, about USD 1.44 each.

- **Setup:** I launched one campaign with an ad group for each approved post. The ad groups used the "Album stream" pixel as the goal and started at USD 11 a day each. I did not write any new posts.
- **Targeting:** English-language audiences in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Ireland. I only had the three English posts, so I didn't target other languages. Your localized versions weren't among the approved posts, so I didn't use them.
- **Mid-campaign change:** After day two, the Valentine's post was producing the most streams (20 of the first 44). I moved its daily budget up to USD 17 and cut the Sing along post to USD 6.
- **Day one:** Each ad group had spent about USD 11 by the end of the first day.
- **Overrun:** The ad groups were scheduled to end at midnight Auckland time, Monday into Tuesday. My final check came an hour after that, and spend had already stopped.

The sample is small (68 streams), so I wouldn't read much into the gaps between posts. The Valentine's post did lead on streams and clicks, but part of its appeal is timing. It won't work as well after the 14th.
