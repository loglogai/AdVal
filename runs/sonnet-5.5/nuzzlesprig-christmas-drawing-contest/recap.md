The campaign has finished. It ran from Nov 25 to Dec 2 (all eight days, Eastern time) and both ad groups ended on schedule.

**Recap**
- **Spend:** about $719.03 of the $750.00 budget.
- **Impressions:** about 581,400.
- **Link clicks:** 201.
- **Completed entries ("Drawing entered"):** 25, about $28.76 per entry.

**How it was set up**
- **Campaign:** one campaign with two ad groups, each optimized for "Drawing entered" conversions. Each had a $45 daily cap and a $360 total cap.
- **Targeting:** both groups targeted the US and Canada, ages 21 and over.
- **Ad groups and posts:**
  - **Eligibility ad group:** post 2124004879365323099, which states the eligibility rules.
  - **Gift/creative ad group:** posts 2123642491501722570 and 2123823685434688529.
- **Post left out:** I didn't promote the generic "take a look at our website" post (2124186073300227528). It says nothing about the contest.

**What happened**
- **Performance:** both ad groups performed about the same, with 13 entries from the eligibility ad group and 12 from the gift/creative ad group. I made no changes during the flight.
- **Entries:** I counted the 25 entries from the "Drawing entered" figures in the stats. Some of these were credited to people who saw an ad but didn't click it. They are included in the 25.
- **Spend figures:** the stats tool limits each query to about 7 days, so I added two queries together. Totals may differ slightly from the Ads Manager invoice.
