The Wrenwell campaign ran from 16 to 27 January (Dublin time) and ended on schedule.

**Totals**
- **Spend:** $315.92 of the $350.00 budget (about $156.86 in the first six days and $159.06 in the last six).
- **Impressions:** 368,907.
- **Link clicks:** 255.

**Installs:** I can't report these. With no X pixel, X can't see them, so your App Store and Google Play numbers are the only count.

**What I ran**
- **Structure:** one campaign with two ad groups, Ireland and the United Kingdom. Each was limited to iPhone and Android and paid per link click.
- **Posts:** all three approved posts ran in both countries.
- **Budget:** each ad group had a $14 daily cap and a $170 total cap.
- **Changes during the run:** none. The ad groups finished within budget and I didn't change them mid-run.

**What the early data showed**
- **Best post:** "Be honest: how many of your invoices are still sitting in drafts?" got the most clicks in the first 36 hours, 19 of the 30 clicks across both countries. That's the only per-post breakdown I pulled, and I didn't shift budget toward it.
- **Weakest post:** the "€0 for three months" post got 3 of the 30 clicks in that window.
- **Countries:** the UK ad group got slightly more impressions and clicks than Ireland at a similar cost.
