**Kovireel Christmas flight recap (Nov 30 – Dec 3, ET)**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Spend | $873.48 of the $950 budget |
| Impressions | 503,992 |
| Link clicks | 166 |
| Cost per link click | about $5.26 |

- **Setup:** I ran one campaign with one ad group, targeting the US, ages 18+, optimized for link clicks. That matches the landing page's eligibility terms.
- **Posts promoted:** I used three of the five approved posts: the double-features post, the "December 24 is the last day" post, and the "14 days free, then $6.99/month" post. I didn't use the other two. The gift-invitation post sends people to share an invitation rather than start a trial, and the generic "thanks for taking a look" post says nothing about the offer.
- **Post performance:** I only pulled per-post numbers for the first two days.
  - The double-features post got the most clicks (40) and the most spend.
  - The Dec 24 deadline post got 26 clicks.
  - The $6.99/month post got 6 clicks on 58,000 impressions, and its cost per click was higher than the double-features post's.
- **What I didn't change:** I never shifted spend between posts. In hindsight I should have paused the weakest post after day 1 or 2.
- **Trial starts:** X can't see them, because your site has no X pixel. Please check your own signup records for trials that started from this campaign. The 166 link clicks are the only conversion-related number I have.
- **Status:** I paused the ad group at the end of the flight (Dec 4, 12:00 AM ET). It is no longer serving.
