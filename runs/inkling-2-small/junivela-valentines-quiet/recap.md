The campaign ran Feb 10–14, 2027 (Bangkok time) and has now ended. Here's the recap.

**Setup**
- One campaign, "Valentine's 2027 — Veranda for Two" (id `zj5nt`), iOS-only platform targeting, worldwide, ALL_ON_TWITTER placement, optimized for link clicks (no app-install objective/pixel exists, and the site has no X pixel, so X cannot see installs).
- Three line items, one per approved post, created and launched as authorized; posts promoted as-is, no new copy written.

**What I changed during the flight**
- Day 2: shifted budget from Post A ("A softer bedtime…") to Post C ("Why does the ceiling…"), which had the best CTR/CPC (A $0.48/click → C $0.37).
- Day 3: paused Post A entirely and raised Post C to $40/day (B held at $20/day), keeping the total at $60/day against the $300 budget.

**Final figures (spend / impressions / link clicks)**
- Post A (paused day 3): $47.71 / 120,669 impressions / 101 link clicks
- Post B ("Send someone a quieter evening…"): $96.86 / 327,650 impressions / 240 link clicks
- Post C ("Why does the ceiling…"): $149.00 / 328,386 impressions / 434 link clicks
- **Total: $293.57 spent / 776,705 impressions / 775 link clicks** (~$0.38 per link click)

**Important distinctions**
- X reports spend, impressions and link clicks only — it has no visibility into installs, pack claims, or page visits, and none of those are counted above. Install totals must come from your own App Store/analytics review.
- The posts and landing page correctly describe the offer as: install and open the iPhone (iOS-only) app by Feb 14, 2027 to keep the Veranda for Two pack free; the pack unlocks automatically on opening the app by the deadline, and it's a shareable link, not a physical gift or voucher — no second account or partner required.
