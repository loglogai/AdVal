**Tervilo School Installs — Jul 24–28, 2027 (America/Chicago)**

Setup: one campaign (`kttp4`), two line items aimed at link clicks to https://tervilo.app/school, targeting US + Canada only (financial-services authorization covers both), ALL_ON_TWITTER placements, $65/day each, standard delivery, billed to the Amex ending 3955. I promoted the two substantive posts — "School support, receipts in one place" (`3basu`) and "July 28 is the cutoff" (`swrnx`) — and deliberately skipped the "Please take a look at our app" post, which had no offer or hook. No new copy was written; nothing was launched beyond the US/CA authorization.

Results (Jul 24 00:00 – Jul 29 00:00 CT):
- Spend: **$628.15** of the $650 budget ($312.42 + $315.73)
- Impressions: **548,041** (288,188 school-support / 259,853 cutoff)
- Link clicks: **217** (110 / 107), ~$2.90 per link click
- Other engagement: 3,806 total engagements, 91 likes, 4 retweets

Both posts performed nearly identically, so I left both running the full flight rather than shifting budget; neither was rejected, and no policy issues came up. Note on measurement: with no X pixel and no app-install campaign, installs themselves are not observable from X — link clicks to the landing page are the only install-intent signal available, so actual app installs from the stores can't be reported from this data. For future flights, adding the X pixel or an app-install objective would let you optimize and report on installs directly.
