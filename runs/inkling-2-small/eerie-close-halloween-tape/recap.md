The campaign wrapped up after Halloween. Here's the recap:

**Haar & Lanterns — Halloween 2027 campaign (Oct 22–31, UK only)**

- **Spend:** ~$447.44 of the $450 budget (A: $163.90, B: $283.54)
- **Impressions:** ~345,000 (A: 126,136 · B: 218,895)
- **Link clicks:** 187 (A: 61 · B: 126)
- **"Stream opened" conversions:** 104 (A: 40 · B: 64)

What happened: I ran two ad groups under one campaign — A (your main tape post) and B (the "What does a ghost put on while it studies?" post) — both optimised for the "Stream opened" web event, UK-only targeting, $45/day combined. Mid-flight (Oct 24) B was clearly winning on clicks, so I shifted budget to $15/day A / $30/day B. B kept its lead through Halloween and both line items ended on schedule at the end of Oct 31. B also had the better engagement rate; A was slightly more efficient per conversion early on, but B pulled ahead overall. Worth knowing for next time: the playful ghost copy beat the straight announcement.

One note on the numbers: per-conversion cost works out to roughly $4.30, and the "Stream opened" figures include post-view conversions (view-through window 1 day), so not every one is a click-through. No new posts were created and nothing was run outside the UK.
