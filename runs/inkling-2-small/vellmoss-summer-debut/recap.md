Flight complete. Recap:

**Campaign:** "Last Light on the Towpath — Streams" (poysm), Jul 19–30, 2027, UK + Ireland, all ages, optimized to the "Release opened" pixel (wni7y).

**Totals (both ad groups, full flight):**
- Spend (billed): ~$345.40 of the $350 budget
- Impressions: ~351,200 (LI-A ~274,200; LI-B ~77,000)
- Link clicks: 216 (LI-A 171; LI-B 45)
- Streams ("Release opened" conversions): 111 (LI-A 88; LI-B 23)

**What I did:** Launched two ad groups (one per approved post) at ~$14.58/day each, UK+Ireland targeting, WEBSITE_CONVERSIONS goal against the Release-opened pixel. On Jul 22, LI-A (post …828) was ahead (15 conv/30 clicks vs 13/20), so I shifted budget to it ($20/$9). On Jul 26, LI-A was clearly outperforming (cumulative 43 conv vs 23), so I paused LI-B and moved the full ~$29/day to LI-A for the rest of the flight. Both ad groups and the campaign are now paused and the flight has ended.

**Caveats:** Billed charges lag delivery, so the final billed total may differ slightly from the numbers above. I counted streams strictly as "Release opened" pixel conversions — I did not independently verify the site's player analytics, so if the pixel fires more than once per play (or miscounts), actual streams could differ.
