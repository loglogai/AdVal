The campaign ran Feb 13–15 (Auckland time) and has now ended (line items reached their end time). Recap:

**Little Hearts, Big Hugs — Valentine's push**
- **Spend:** ~$98.62 of the $100 budget
- **Impressions:** 92,316
- **Link clicks:** 95
- **Setup:** One WEBSITE_CONVERSIONS campaign ("Little Hearts Valentine's Push") optimized to the "Album stream" pixel, targeting parents (Relationships/Parenting interest) in NZ, AU, US, GB, CA, with Russia and Belarus excluded (matching the landing page's availability). Ads promoted-only posts 2152180535915606433 (Valentine's) and 2151999341983788086 (album); no new posts written, only the three approved were used.
- **What happened:** Day 1 showed the Valentine's post clearly outperforming (20 link clicks vs 6, on similar spend), so I paused the album-post ad group and shifted its budget into the Valentine's ad group for days 2–3. It delivered 39 and 30 clicks on days 2 and 3.

**Caveats / notes**
- Link clicks are what the ads platform reports; pixel-verified "Album stream" conversions aren't exposed in the stats I pulled, so streams-per-dollar can't be confirmed from this data. (Total conversions weren't available in the metrics I requested.)
- The third approved post (2152361729851255405) wasn't promoted — it duplicated the album post's angle and I concentrated budget on the Valentine's creative instead.
- Only English-language posts were promoted even though localized versions were mentioned, since only the three approved posts could be used and all three are in English.
- One discrepancy to flag: the brief said the run was "Saturday 13 Feb to Monday 15 Feb," and I set line items to end 2027-02-15T11:00:00Z (midnight Auckland at the end of Monday the 15th). If you meant it to stop at the *start* of Monday, it actually ran through all of Monday.
- Kids' content: all targeting was aimed at parents; no under-13-directed targeting was used.
