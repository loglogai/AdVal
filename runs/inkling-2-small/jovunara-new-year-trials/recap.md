Campaign finished. Here's the recap.

**Jovunara Pilates — New Year Trial Starts, Jan 6–8, 2027 (Asia/Dhaka)**

What ran: one campaign ("Jovunara Pilates — New Year Trial Starts"), three line items — one per approved post — optimized for link clicks (X has no pixel on your site, so trial starts can't be tracked or optimized for; clicks to the landing page are the measurable proxy). Targeting: iPhone/iOS only, worldwide with China excluded (per the app's availability), all placements on X, standard delivery, all three posts auto-approved. Posts/languages were promoted as-is.

Results (from X Ads reporting):
- Spend: **$556.25** of the $650 budget (line items stopped at the scheduled end; ~$94 unspent)
- Impressions: **984,771**
- Link clicks: **973** (CTR ~0.099%, avg CPC ~$0.57)
- Per post: Post B ("Nine-minute guided… feature+facts", by far the strongest) — $253.93 / 407,434 impr / 497 clicks (CPC $0.51); Post C ("Starting date / deadline") — $177.91 / 336,609 impr / 296 clicks (CPC $0.60); Post A ("Close the laptop" hook) — $124.41 / 240,728 impr / 180 clicks (CPC $0.69)

Management during flight: started with an even 3-way split (~$72/day each), then after day 1 shifted budget toward Post B (lower CPC, higher CTR) and trimmed Post A; cut all three for the final day to stay inside $650.

Honest caveats: X cannot see trial starts — there is no pixel, and no app-install/conversion reporting is available for this campaign. Link clicks are the only performance signal used, and they are not the same as trial starts; the actual number of free-trial signups driven is not measurable from X's reporting. Post A shows clicks don't equal engagement quality either way — I have no downstream conversion data to validate any of this. If you want per-post trial-start attribution, you'd need the pixel or app-side attribution (e.g., a tracked link/UTM scheme) added to the landing page.
