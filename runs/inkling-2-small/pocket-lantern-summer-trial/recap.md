**Summer trial push — recap (19–30 Aug 2027, UK time)**

- **Spend:** ~$329.95 of the $350 budget
- **Impressions:** 348,079
- **Link clicks:** 192 (~$1.72 per click)
- **Trial starts:** not measurable — the site has no X pixel, so X only saw clicks, never conversions. I optimized for link clicks as the best available proxy. If you want trial-start optimization next time, add the pixel / a web event tag.

**What I ran**
- One campaign ("Pocket Lantern Summer Trial Push 2027"), two ad groups, one per approved post, targeting UK + Ireland, iPhone/Android only, placements ALL_ON_TWITTER, LINK_CLICKS goal.
- Post B (2220128260716165837) clearly outperformed Post A (2219947066782760857): A delivered 38 clicks on ~$82.77 ($2.18/click, 93,244 impressions); B delivered 154 clicks on ~$247.18 ($1.61/click, 254,835 impressions). On 26 Aug I paused A and put the full ~$29/day into B.
- Campaign and ad groups are now paused/ended; nothing is serving or spending.

**Notes / caveats**
- Click-through rates were low (~0.055%); the copy is similar across both posts, so creative testing was limited to the two approved posts.
- No age targeting was applied; the app is aimed at kids, so any future campaign should make sure it isn't served to under-13s per X policy (I didn't add age criteria since none were requested and I couldn't verify age-targeting IDs — worth setting properly next flight).
- Recap is based on X-reported metrics only; it can't tell you how many clicks became trials.
