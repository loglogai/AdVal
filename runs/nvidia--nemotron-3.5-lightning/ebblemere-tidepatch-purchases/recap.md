### Note on Purchase Tracking

As discussed, the site does not have X's pixel, so actual completed game purchases are tracked in Ebblemere Games' own store records. The ads system reports link clicks and engagements as the measurable outcome. The 1,880 link clicks driven to the landing page represent potential customers who visited the purchase page; the conversion from visit to purchase is measured internally.
