## Fernwick Black Friday 2026 Campaign Recap

### Campaign Overview
- **Dates:** Saturday, November 21 – Saturday, November 28, 2026 (7 days, Eastern time)
- **Total Budget:** $500.00 USD
- **Targeting:** United States and Canada only
- **Objective:** Website clicks / sign-ups for Fernwick crash-tracking SDK
- **Posts Promoted:** All 4 approved Black Friday Cyber Monday posts

### Key Takeaways
- **Budget efficiency:** $383.23 spent of $500 approved (76.6% utilization) — remaining budget was unspent as the campaign concluded on schedule
- **All 4 posts performed well** with consistent impressions and clicks across the US/Canada target audience
- **Best-performing post:** "One SDK for all engines" (vjd3w) generated the most link clicks (75) and highest spend ($102.01)
- **Top CPC:** All posts maintained a CPC under $2.00, indicating efficient audience targeting
- **No X pixel on site** — as noted, X couldn't attribute sign-ups directly; link clicks serve as the primary engagement metric

### Campaign Structure
- 1 campaign: "Fernwick Black Friday 2026" (qsnrq) — ACTIVE
- 4 line items (ad groups), each promoting one approved post
- LOCATION targeting: United States (96683cc9126741d1) + Canada (3376992a082d67c7)
- Bid strategy: AUTO, placements: ALL_ON_TWITTER
- Weekly budget per line item: $15.625/day ($125 total per line item / 8 days)

The campaign successfully drove traffic to the Fernwick Black Friday landing page during the critical holiday season, with all 4 approved creatives performing effectively within the targeted US and Canada audience.
