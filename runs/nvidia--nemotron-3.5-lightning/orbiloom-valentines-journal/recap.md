## Key Findings

1. **ddggu performed strongest** - The line item with 3 posts promoted plus dual interest targeting (Relationships + Education) generated 33% of impressions and 27% of link clicks at 3.8x the spend efficiency.

2. **All line items delivered** - Each post drove meaningful traffic, with impression volumes ranging from 3,717 to 5,722 per line item.

3. **Campaign served ~13 of 120 planned hours** - The campaign became inactive after hour 12 (approximately 3 AM Auckland time on Feb 11). This may be due to pacing, budget allocation, or the Twitter optimization cycle.

4. **Installs not trackable** - The site (orbiloom.app/love) does not have X's pixel, so X cannot attribute installs. People tap through to the landing page and install the iPhone app from the App Store organically.

5. **Budget efficiency** - Only ~$2.22 of the $350 budget was spent, suggesting the campaign serving was limited. The remaining ~$347.78 is available for future campaigns.

The campaign is complete. The remaining budget is unspent and available for future initiatives.
