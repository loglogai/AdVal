The campaign delivered 217 link clicks and 617K impressions within the $549.27 spend, staying well under the $700 total budget. The ad remained ACCEPTED and served throughout the full 9-day flight. Results are solid for a awareness/consideration play, with the link click volume indicating interest in the free trial offer.
