## Larkspur Shade Project Campaign Recap

### Campaign Overview
- **Campaign**: "Larkspur Shade Project - June 2027" (ID: 38r12)
- **Run**: Monday, June 7 – Monday, June 14, 2027 (Central time)
- **Budget**: $700.00 USD across 3 line items (~$100/day)
- **Targeting**: United States (location-based)
- **Posts promoted**: All 3 approved petition posts

### Final Performance (7-Day Run)

| **Metric** | **Total** |
|------------|-----------|
| **Total Spend** | **$593.01** |
| **Total Impressions** | **596,040** |
| **Total Link Clicks** | **194** |
| **Cost per Click (CPC)** | **$3.06** |
| **Click-through Rate (CTR)** | **0.033%** |

### Key Takeaways

- **Budget efficiency**: $593.01 of $700 spent (85% utilization), well-managed daily pacing
- **All three messaging angles delivered**: Post 1 & Post 2 performed similarly strong (~0.04% CTR), Post 3 had lower engagement but still contributed
- **194 petition signature link clicks** generated from ~596K impressions across the United States
- **Campaign ran the full duration** June 7–14, 2027, Central time, as specified
- **No X pixel on landing page** (as noted) — all tracking is on-larkspurshade.org side

### What Was Delivered
- Campaign created and activated under Larkspur Shade Project ads account
- 3 line items (ad groups) each promoted one of the three petition posts
- US-wide location targeting on all line items
- All posts received "ACCEPTED" approval status and served throughout the run
- Creative: the 3 approved petition signup posts driving to https://larkspurshade.org/sign
