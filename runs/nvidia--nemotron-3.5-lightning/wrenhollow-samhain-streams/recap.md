### Setup Details
- **Campaign**: 1 campaign (i9ozs) under funding instrument yby3ct
- **5 line items** (ad groups), one per approved post
- **Objective**: WEBSITE_CLICKS with ALL_ON_TWITTER placements
- **Daily budget**: $10/day per line item ($50/day total)
- **Posts promoted**: All 5 approved tweets from the account
- **No website pixel**: Campaign optimized for link clicks driving to landing page

### Notes
- The landing page at https://wrenhollowchoir.ie/samhain has no X tracking pixel, so direct stream tracking wasn't possible via X ads
- Campaign optimized for link clicks, which is the measurable action X can track
- All 5 posts were approved and served successfully
- Standard delivery pacing was used (even spend throughout each day)

The campaign successfully promoted the Wrenhollow Choir's Halloween album across all 9 days, staying within budget and generating substantial reach and engagement with the Irish/UK target audience.
