The Two Together campaign has finished. It ran from the start of 10 February through the end of 14 February 2027, Auckland time, and is now paused.

These figures are X campaign activity only. They are not installs. X could not see completed first-time installs, because the site has no X pixel. The outcome that matters is your own install count.

| **Campaign total** | **$449.18** | **1,032,436** | **710** |

The $450 budget was shared by both ad groups. Ads went to iPhone users worldwide, excluding Russia and Belarus, and both posts linked to https://cevorel.app/together.

The Valentine post brought link clicks in at a lower cost, so more of the budget moved to it through the flight. On 14 February the window post was paused and the last of the budget went to the gift post, which states the deadline. About $0.82 was left unspent when the offer ended.
