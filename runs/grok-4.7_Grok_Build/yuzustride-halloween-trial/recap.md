The Halloween flight is finished. It spent **$696.29** of the $700 budget, with **439,254** impressions and **317** link clicks to https://yuzustride.com/halloween.

X does not record trial starts, because the site has no pixel. Those 317 link clicks are the measured result, from people on iPhone and Android in the US and Canada.

The relay post carried the flight after the first day and a half. It delivered 304 link clicks and $648.25 in spend. The deadline post delivered 13 link clicks at a higher cost per click, so that ad group was paused on October 24 and its remaining budget moved to the relay post. The campaign ended on its own at the close of October 31, Eastern time.
