The Tidelines campaign has finished. It ran from Thursday 11 February through the end of Monday 15 February, Perth time, and is now expired.

**Spend:** $199.29 USD
**Impressions:** 777,000
**Link clicks:** 509

All of that went to https://marrowlanescores.com.au/tidelines. X has no pixel on the site, so these are link clicks only. Your streaming dashboards are the stream count.

The "$0" post was the most efficient every day, including Valentine's Day, so it took the largest share of the budget. "Send them a song" cost the most per click and was paused after Friday. The pelican post ran through Sunday and was paused for Monday. The generic "Hope you enjoy it" post was not promoted.

Ads ran worldwide. Iran, North Korea, Cuba, and Syria are not in X's location list, so they could not be targeted.
