The Bracket Bust campaign is finished. It ran from Tuesday, March 30 through midnight Monday, April 5, Central time, and then expired on its own.

**Spend:** $548.84 of the $550 budget
**Impressions:** 441,509
**Link clicks:** 297

That is about $1.85 per link click. These are clicks to https://quarterlineseltzer.com/bracket. X could not see entries, since the pixel is not on the site, so you can match these 297 clicks against your entry count.
