The Valentine's push for Little Hearts, Big Hugs has ended. It ran Saturday 13 February through Monday 15 February 2027, Auckland time, and the campaign is now expired.

**Spend:** $100.00
**Impressions:** 222,072
**Link clicks:** 246

The Album stream pixel recorded 111 streams (106 after a click, 5 after a view).

Almost all of the budget went to the Valentine's post, which produced 105 of those streams. The new-album post was paused on Sunday after a higher cost per stream, and the specs post was paused on Saturday. Russia and Belarus were excluded, and ads were limited to people 18 and older.
