Havlumi's Valentine flight is finished. It ran from Monday, February 8 through Sunday, February 14, 2027, Singapore time, and the campaign is now expired, so it is no longer serving.

X did not record app installs. There is no X pixel and no app-install campaign. The figures below are ad spend, impressions, and link clicks to https://havlumi.app/valentine. A link click only means someone opened that page. Completed iPhone installs are counted in Havlumi's own app reporting.

**Flight total:** $545.11 spent of the $550.00 budget, 1,427,827 impressions, and 951 link clicks. The average cost was $0.57 per link click.

By Singapore day, link clicks were 124, 120, 121, 129, 136, 162, and 159 from February 8 through February 14. Spend stayed inside the daily plan and finished $4.89 under the cap.

The how-it-works post was the most efficient all week: the lowest cost per link click and the highest share of people who tapped through. After the first day, and again after day three, its budget was increased. On February 13 and 14 it carried most of the spend and produced 108 and 102 link clicks. The story-strip post stayed in the middle. The Valentine keepsake post cost the most per link click, so its budget was reduced, and it stayed on through February 14 because it is the only approved post that states the keepsake-pack deadline.

Ads ran on X to iOS devices worldwide, with China excluded. X's platform target covers iOS, including iPad; there was no separate iPhone-only control. The posts and the landing page say the app is iPhone only.
