Samhain Night Songs finished at midnight on 31 October, Dublin time. The campaign spent **$444.38** of the $450 budget, for **469,006 impressions** and **318 link clicks** to https://wrenhollowchoir.ie/samhain.

X could not count streams, because the site has no X pixel. Link clicks are the result it can report.

Most of the clicks came from the plastic-pumpkins post shown to people in Ireland and the UK who were already talking about Halloween and Samhain. The free-to-stream post ran alongside it for music fans in the UK. The vague “hope you enjoy it” post was not promoted.
