The back-to-school campaign has finished. It ran from Friday 30 July 2027 through the end of Tuesday 10 August 2027, Irish time, and the campaign is now expired.

**Spend:** $399.81 of the $400 budget
**Impressions:** 378,434
**Link clicks:** 193 to https://brackenhold.ie/trial

That is about $2.07 per link click. Trial starts are not in these figures, because X has no pixel on the site. Those stay in your own count.

All four approved posts ran, aimed at Ireland and the United Kingdom on iPhone and Android, among people already talking about climbing. After the first few days, the “back on the wall” post was bringing trial-page clicks at a lower cost than the term, plan, and price posts, so the rest of the budget went to that post.
