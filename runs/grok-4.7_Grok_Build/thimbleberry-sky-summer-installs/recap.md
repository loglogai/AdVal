The summer push has wrapped. The campaign ran from Tuesday, July 13 through Tuesday, July 20, 2027 Pacific time, and it is now finished and no longer spending.

**Spend:** $748.09
**Impressions:** 665,814
**Link clicks:** 344

That was one campaign, Summer 2027 iPhone installs, aimed at English-speaking iOS users in the United States and Canada and optimized for the “App installed” pixel. All five approved posts ran against the shared $750 budget.

The $0 post and the iPhone / US & Canada post did the work, so the later budget went to those two. The Perseids post was paused on July 14, and the “point your iPhone” and “bright planet” posts were paused on July 16, after they were bringing in fewer installs for the spend.
