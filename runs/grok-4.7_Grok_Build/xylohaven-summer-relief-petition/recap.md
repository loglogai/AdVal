The Summer Relief Petition flight is finished. It ran July 16–20 Pacific time, spent **$999.91**, and delivered **704,454 impressions** and **243 link clicks** to https://xylohavencivic.org/summer-relief.

X has no pixel on the site, so completed signatures are not in these numbers. Link clicks are the closest count of people sent to the petition.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Errands restroom post | $671.78 | 422,469 | 166 |
| July 20 deadline post | $328.13 | 281,985 | 77 |
| **Total** | **$999.91** | **704,454** | **243** |

Both posts ran as website-click ads to US residents 18 and older. Through Sunday the errands post kept a lower cost per click ($4.05 overall vs. $4.26) and a higher click rate. On Monday afternoon the deadline ad group was paused and the remaining budget moved to the errands post. That post then took Monday and Tuesday, including 49 link clicks on the last day. The campaign has expired and is no longer spending. About $0.09 of the $1,000 budget was left.
