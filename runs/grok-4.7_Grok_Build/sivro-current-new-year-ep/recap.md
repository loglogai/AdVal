The One More Morning campaign has finished. It ran from 5 January through 17 January 2027 in Johannesburg time and is now expired, so it will not spend any further.

**Spend:** $99.75 of the $100.00 budget  
**Impressions:** 422,834  
**Link clicks:** 433  

Those 433 link clicks are people who opened https://sivrocurrent.com/one-more-morning. They are not streams. A stream only counts when someone then opens the release on Sivro Stream. The site has no X pixel, so X could not see or optimize for that step, and this recap does not include a stream count.

The campaign paid for link clicks and ran only the posts that tell people to open Sivro Stream or stream the EP. It stayed on people interested in electronic music, everywhere except China. The vague “please take a look” post was not promoted. After the first days, the “press play” post and later the resolutions post were paused because their cost per link click was higher, and the remaining budget went to the two clearer stream posts.
