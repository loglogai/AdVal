The Larkspur Shade petition campaign has finished. It ran from 1:00 a.m. Central on Monday, June 7 through the end of Monday, June 14, and it is now expired.

**Spend:** $693.51 of the $700 budget
**Impressions:** 611,780
**Link clicks:** 252
**Cost per link click:** $2.75

All three approved posts ran to https://larkspurshade.org/sign, aimed at people in the United States age 18 and older. There is no X pixel on the site, so these figures are link clicks, not petition signatures. Your own signature count is the one to use for the Metro Council case.

| Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Brutal summer | $460.77 | 373,830 | 168 | $2.74 |
| Whole ask | $168.21 | 179,089 | 63 | $2.67 |
| Summer heat | $64.52 | 58,861 | 21 | $3.07 |

Summer heat cost more per click after the first two days, so that ad group was paused on June 9 and the rest of its budget went to the other two posts. Brutal summer took most of that budget because it kept spending at a similar cost per click. About $6.49 was still unspent when the flight ended.
