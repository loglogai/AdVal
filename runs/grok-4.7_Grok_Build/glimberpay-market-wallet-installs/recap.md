The campaign ran from 27 June through 9 July 2027, UK time, and has ended. It spent **$399.72** of the $400 budget.

| | |
|---|---|
| Spend | $399.72 |
| Impressions | 331,869 |
| Link clicks | 163 |
| Wallet installed | 18 |

Ads went to adults in the United Kingdom on iOS and Android, and optimized for the verified “Wallet installed” event. People reached the market-wallet page from the approved posts and installed from there.

The post that carried the result was the QR one: pay by QR at participating UK stalls, free download, no monthly wallet fee, receipt sorting free for 30 days if installed by 9 July, and phones only. After the first few days it was the only post producing click-attributed installs (11 of them, plus 5 view-through). From 1 July the remaining budget went to that post. It finished at $297.75, 244,668 impressions, 118 link clicks and 16 installs.

The other four posts shared about $102 in the opening days. The receipt story drew the most early clicks (19) and one view-through install. “Payments in one wallet” also had one view-through install. The £0 download post and the 9 July deadline post recorded no installs, so those four were paused on 1 July.

The 14-day click window on “Wallet installed” can still add a few installs from clicks made before the flight ended. View-through installs are already closed (1-day window).
