The summer school-streets campaign has finished. It ran from 24 June through 4 July 2027, UK time, and is now expired.

**Spend:** $447.51 of the $450 budget  
**Impressions:** 350,461  
**Link clicks:** 217  

Ads went to United Kingdom residents aged 18 and over, and optimized for the “Petition signed” pixel on https://gatelarkstreets.org/sign. The “call for 20 mph” post took most of the budget after it produced signatures more cheaply. “Closes 4 July” was paused on 25 June with no signatures, and the school-gate post was paused on 27 June after its clicks rarely turned into signatures. The $2.49 left unspent was unused room on those paused ad groups.
