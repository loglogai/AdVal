Haar & Lanterns ran in the UK from 22 October through the end of Halloween and has now finished. It spent **$442.45** of the $450 budget.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tape announcement (music fans) | $318.40 | 184,525 | 119 |
| Tape announcement (study keywords) | $88.91 | 61,398 | 38 |
| Ghost studies post | $35.14 | 19,984 | 12 |
| **Total** | **$442.45** | **265,907** | **169** |

Those clicks led to **127** “Stream opened” events. The descriptive tape post did almost all of the work. The study-keyword version of that same post was the most efficient, so more of the budget went there as the week went on. The ghost post picked up a few streams early, then stopped converting, so it was paused on 24 October.
