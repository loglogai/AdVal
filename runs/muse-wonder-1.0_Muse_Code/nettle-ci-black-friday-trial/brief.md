We're Nettle CI, a team of six working out of a converted mill near Ancoats in Manchester. The product is simple to describe: it connects to your CI (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, CircleCI, Jenkins), reads back through your run history, and gives every test a flakiness score based on how often it passes and fails on the same commit, how retries behave, and whether failures cluster around particular runners or times of day. You get a ranked list of the tests that waste the most of your team's time, and a weekly note on which ones got better or worse. We don't quarantine anything automatically. We just tell you, with evidence, which red builds are lying to you.
