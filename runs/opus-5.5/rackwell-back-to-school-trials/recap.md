## Rackwell back-to-school push recap (Jul 28–31, 2027)

The campaign ran the full flight and stopped on schedule at 11:59 PM Central on Saturday, July 31. Spend came in under the $450 budget.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$448.66** | **351,231** | **245** | **$1.83** |

**By post:**
| Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Be honest: how many of your dumbbells…" | $196.75 | 109,884 | 108 | $1.82 |
| "Rackwell plans your week…" | $95.35 | 85,403 | 53 | $1.80 |
| "Back to school, back to lifting…" | $95.18 | 84,354 | 51 | $1.87 |
| "Classes start soon…" | $61.38 | 71,590 | 33 | $1.86 |

**Setup:** I ran one campaign with one ad group. It targeted the whole United States on iPhone only, with optimized targeting off so ads stayed inside that audience. It was set to get as many clicks to rackwellapp.com/trial as possible, because X can't see trial starts without a pixel. Spend was capped at $450 total and $112.50 per day. All four ads were approved right away.

**Not used:** I left out the "We have an app!" post. It links to the homepage instead of /trial and doesn't mention the free trial.

**What I saw:** I checked about every 12–24 hours. Cost per click was nearly the same for all four posts, between $1.80 and $1.87. The "dumbbells holding a door open" post got the most delivery and the most clicks (44% of the total). None of the posts was clearly worse, so I didn't pause any or move budget between them.

**Trial starts:** X can't measure them, so check how many trials started in your own app or store data for Jul 28–31. Dividing $448.66 by that number gives your cost per trial. If you add the X pixel to /trial before your next push, X can optimize for trial starts directly instead of clicks.
