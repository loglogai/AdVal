# Eldergulch Tapes S3: campaign recap (Oct 24–31, 2027)

**Spend: $695.38** of the $700 budget
**Impressions: 662,686**
**Link clicks: 329** (about $2.11 per click)

The campaign ended on schedule at midnight Mountain Time on Halloween night. It shows as expired and can't spend anything more.

| Ad group (US only) | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Horror & Thriller + Podcasts interests | $332.65 | 208,960 | 147 | $2.26 |
| Broad (no interests) | $362.73 | 453,726 | 182 | $1.99 |

**Streams:** X can't see streams without its pixel on your site, so I set the ads to get as many clicks to eldergulch.com/season3 as possible. That's the nearest step to a stream that X can measure. The 329 clicks are visits to your landing page, not plays. Your Spotify and Apple Podcasts numbers are the real test. If you compare them by day, the busiest ad days were Oct 27, 30 and 31.

**What I did:**
- I ran all four approved posts in both ad groups. Each got 79–88 clicks over the week, so no post clearly won and I never paused any.
- From day 3, the Broad group was bringing in clicks more cheaply. I moved some budget from the interest group into it, while keeping the total cap at $700.
- On Halloween I raised both daily budgets so the money left over got spent on the night that mattered most.

**For next time:** If you add X's pixel to eldergulch.com and track clicks out to the listening apps as a conversion, X can optimize for people who actually head off to listen, not just people who click.

Hope the tunnel take behaved itself. 🎧
