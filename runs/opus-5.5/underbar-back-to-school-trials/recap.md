# Underbar Back to School recap (Aug 4–7, 2027)

The campaign ran all four days and is finished. It spent **$543.11 of the $550.00 budget**, so it came in $6.89 under.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$543.11** | **463,042** | **256** | **$2.12** |
| Broad ad group (US + Canada, iOS) | $386.81 | 387,170 | 193 | $2.00 |
| Fitness-interest ad group (US + Canada, iOS) | $156.30 | 75,872 | 63 | $2.48 |

**Setup:** One campaign with two ad groups, both limited to the US and Canada on iOS. One reached everyone on iOS in those countries; the other only reached people interested in Health & Fitness or Workouts. Both promoted all 5 of your approved posts. No new posts were written. Because X can't see your trial starts, I set the ad groups to get as many link clicks to underbar.app/backtoschool as possible. That was the closest thing X could measure.

**Changes as it ran:**
- **After day 1:** Each click from the fitness group cost about 25% more than one from the broad group. I moved budget toward the broad group.
- **After day 3:** Fitness clicks had dropped from 30 to 19 to 13 a day, while the broad group held steady at about 45 a day. I paused the fitness group and put the rest of the budget on the broad group for the final day (Aug 7, the last day of the 14-day offer).
- **After it ended:** I also paused the campaign, which had already expired, so nothing can serve by accident.

**How the posts did (both ad groups combined):**

| Post | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|
| "Be honest: how many pull-ups…" | 92 | $2.15 |
| "14 days free. That's the whole pitch." | 72 | $2.06 |
| "Classes start soon…" | 40 | $2.13 |
| "Your first pull-up is closer…" | 28 | $2.21 |
| "Underbar turns calisthenics into levels…" | 24 | $2.10 |

The cost per click was nearly the same for every post ($2.06–$2.21), so none of them clearly did better. I left all five running. The "Be honest" and "14 days free" posts got the most clicks because X showed them more often.

**What I can't tell you:** how many of those 256 clicks became trial starts. Only your own tracking has that. If you check trial starts against those four days, you'll know your real cost per trial. If the broad group's cheaper clicks also turned into trials, keep it as the starting point next time. Adding X's pixel would let X aim directly at trial starts instead of clicks.
