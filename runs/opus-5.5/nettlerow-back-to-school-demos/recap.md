**Nettlerow back-to-school campaign: recap (6–19 Aug, Ireland + UK only)**

- **Spend:** $337.89 of the $350 budget
- **Impressions:** 349,289
- **Link clicks to nettlerow.ie/demo:** 143, about $2.36 each

**What worked**
- **Pharmacy keyword targeting** (pharmacist, community pharmacy, independent pharmacy, Irish Pharmacy Union, NPA and similar) did best: 88 clicks for $204, about $2.32 each.
- **Accounts similar to your followers:** 55 clicks for $133, about $2.43 each.
- **Best post:** "Two weeks until the school-run queue…" got roughly twice the clicks of the lice/paracetamol post. The €89/£79 price post got very few clicks. That comparison covers 6–11 Aug only; I tracked the ad groups rather than individual posts after that.

**Changes during the run:** On 11 Aug I added more pharmacy keywords. On 13 Aug I raised the daily caps so the budget would be used in full.

The campaign has ended and nothing is still running. X can't track demo bookings without the pixel, so your calendar is the real result. Divide $337.89 by the number of demos booked to get your cost per demo.
