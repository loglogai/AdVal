# Ovelaro New Year campaign recap: Dec 30, 2026 – Jan 12, 2027 (Lagos time)

| | Total |
|---|---|
| **Spend** | **$350.00** of the $350.00 budget |
| **Impressions** | **467,820** |
| **Link clicks** | **235** |
| Installs ("App installed" pixel) | 36, about $9.72 per install |

**Setup:** I ran one campaign that optimised for your "App installed" pixel event. It targeted only Nigeria, Ghana and South Africa, and only iOS and Android phones. All three posts passed X's review and served. I didn't write any new posts. The campaign and ad groups are now paused and can't spend anything more.

**What changed during the flight:**
- **Dec 30 – Jan 7:** All three posts ran in one ad group, which spent $223.73.
- **Jan 8:** I stopped promoting post 3 (the "January 12 is the cutoff" post). It had been getting about 2–3 times fewer link clicks per impression than the other two. Over Jan 5–7 it got 5 link clicks and 1 install from $19.69, while posts 1 and 2 got 25 link clicks each.
- **Jan 8 – 12:** Posts 1 and 2 ran in a new ad group capped at $126, so total spend couldn't go over $350. It spent $125.30.

**Results by ad group:**
- All three posts: $223.73 spent, 302,457 impressions, 163 link clicks, 27 installs (about $8.29 each).
- Posts 1 and 2 only: $125.30 spent, 165,363 impressions, 72 link clicks, 9 installs (about $13.92 each). Installs got more expensive late in the flight even with the weaker post removed. That's probably because the new ad group had to start learning from scratch, and fewer people were looking to install as the Jan 12 deadline got close.

**Posts:** Post 2 ("actually take that trip") got the most installs per dollar in the first ad group. Post 1 (the resolution and no-service-fee offer) got the most link clicks. Post 3 consistently did worst on both.

**Notes on the numbers:**
- Installs come from X's pixel, and 11 of the 36 were counted after someone only saw an ad, without clicking it. Check this against your own install tracking.
- Attribution is still open, so a few late installs may still be added.
