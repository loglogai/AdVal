**How it was set up:** I used one campaign with one ad group. It targeted every country X lets us reach (Iran, North Korea, Cuba and Syria aren't among them), people aged 18+, and English speakers. All three approved posts ran and passed review. Cost per click was about the same for all three, so I didn't pause any. The "to-do list" post got the most delivery.

**What to keep in mind:**
- **I couldn't optimize for purchases.** Without X's pixel on your site, X can't see sales, so the campaign aimed for landing-page clicks, the closest result X can measure. To see how many purchases it drove, check your store records for Apr 29 – May 2. Adding the pixel next time would let X aim for completed purchases directly.
- **Only English-speaking audiences saw the ads.** All three approved posts are in English, so I targeted English speakers only. That leaves out the other-language markets you mentioned. If you approve the translated versions next time, I can run them in their own markets.
