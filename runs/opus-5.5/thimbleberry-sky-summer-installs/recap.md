# Thimbleberry Sky summer push: recap (Jul 13–20, 2027, Pacific time)

The campaign spent **$749.89 of your $750**, got **672,441 impressions** and **348 link clicks**, and X recorded **36 "App installed" events**. Both ad groups reached their July 20 end date (midnight Pacific) and are no longer serving.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Installs | Cost per install |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ad group 1: all 5 posts (Jul 13–16) | $386.58 | 333,078 | 161 | 17 | ~$22.74 |
| Ad group 2: best 3 posts (Jul 17–20) | $363.32 | 339,363 | 187 | 19 | ~$19.12 |
| **Total** | **$749.89** | **672,441** | **348** | **36** | **~$20.83** |

**How I set it up:** one campaign optimizing for your "App installed" pixel event. It was limited to iPhone (iOS) users in the US and Canada, with only your 5 approved posts as the ads. All 5 were approved by X.

**What changed along the way:** after 4 days, three posts had brought in clearly more installs:
- "$0. That's what it costs…" (6 installs)
- "Thimbleberry Sky names stars, planets & constellations…" (4)
- "That super bright 'star'…" (3)

The "Point your iPhone…" and "Perseids season…" posts had 2 installs each on about the same spend. On July 17 I paused the first ad group and moved the remaining budget to a new one running only the top 3 posts. Its cost per install was about 16% lower.

**Notes:**
- Ad group 1 spent $0.58 more than the $386 limit I set when I paused it. Spend was a little past that figure by the time the pause took effect. Total spend still stayed under $750.
- The 36 installs are what your pixel reported to X. 30 came from people who clicked an ad. The other 6 came from people who saw an ad without clicking and installed within a day. Your App Store numbers may differ a bit.
