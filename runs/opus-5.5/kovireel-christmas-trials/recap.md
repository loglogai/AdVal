**What I set up**
- **One campaign with two ad groups**, both limited to US users aged 18+ to match the trial's eligibility rules. Each had a $125/day cap and a $475 total cap, so combined spend couldn't go over $950.
- **Posts used:** I ran three of your five posts: the double-features post, the "December 24 is the last day" post and the "$6.99/month" post. All were approved and all match the landing page.
- **Posts left out:**
  - The gift-invitation post, because it pushes invitation shares, which you said you don't count.
  - The "Thanks for taking a look" post, because it doesn't mention the offer.

**What I learned**
- **No clear winner, so I made no changes.** At every check, each ad cost between about $4.50 and $5.50 per click.
- **The keyword ad group did best:** about $0.30 cheaper per click than the interest one.
- **The double-features post in the keyword group was the strongest single ad:** about 50 clicks.
- **The "$6.99/month" post drove the fewest clicks.** It may still bring in better-qualified trial starts, since people know the price before they click. Your signup records are the only way to tell.
