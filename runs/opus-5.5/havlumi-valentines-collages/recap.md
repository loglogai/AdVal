# Havlumi Valentine 2027 on X: recap

The campaign ran Monday, Feb 8 through Sunday, Feb 14, 2027, Singapore time. It spent **$547.38 of the $550.00 budget**, delivered **1,455,925 impressions** and drove **976 link clicks**, an average of about $0.56 per link click.

**Link clicks are not installs.** Your site has no X pixel, so X can't see installs. I set the campaign to get as many clicks through to havlumi.app/valentine as possible, since that's the closest result X can measure. Your app reporting for Feb 8–14 is the only record of installs. Divide the $547.38 by that install count to get your real cost per install.

## Setup
- **Structure:** one campaign with one ad group. It ran on X only, targeted iPhone (iOS) users in every country except China, and promoted all three approved posts.
- **Budget:** the ad group had a $550 lifetime cap, so it could not overspend. Daily spend started at a $78.57 limit. On Feb 12 I raised it to $82 to make sure the budget would be used up by Feb 14. Delivery ended at midnight Singapore time on Feb 14, and I then paused the campaign.

## Observations
- **The practical post won.** "Choose three photos…" spells out the steps, the price and the requirements (iPhone only, not in China). It had the best click rate every day and X gave it about half the budget. That fits your plain, practical voice.
- **The Valentine-themed post was the weakest.** It had the lowest click rate, even as Feb 14 got closer. Cost per click was about the same for all three posts, so the differences show up as click rate and how much budget X gave each post.
- **Why I didn't pause any post:** results were steady from day to day, and X was already moving budget to the strongest post. Pausing the weaker two would have narrowed the audience without lowering the cost per click.
- **Language:** all three posts are in English and ran worldwide. I didn't add language targeting, so people outside English-speaking markets saw English text. You mentioned having versions of each post in every market's language. Approving those next time would likely raise click rates outside English-speaking markets.
- **Next time:** install the X pixel, or an app tracking SDK, before launch. Then X could optimize for and report installs directly, instead of using clicks as a stand-in.
