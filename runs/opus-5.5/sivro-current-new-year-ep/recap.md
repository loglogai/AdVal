|---|---|

**These link clicks are not your streams.** A link click means someone tapped through to https://sivrocurrent.com/one-more-morning. Your streams are counted only when someone opens the release on Sivro Stream. Your site doesn't have X's pixel, so X can't see any of that, and I have no stream numbers. To find out how many of the 530 clicks turned into streams, compare your own Sivro Stream stats for Jan 5–17 with the click count. Some visits will come from other places, so treat that comparison as a rough guide.

**What I ran:** one campaign, set to get as many link clicks as possible. It ran worldwide except China, in two audiences:
- people interested in electronic music
- people using keywords like melodic, deep, afro and progressive house, house music and Anjunadeep

**Changes during the flight:**
- **Jan 5:** launched with four of your approved posts in both audiences. I left out "Something new from me…" because it doesn't say what the release is, that it's free or when the free window ends.
- **Jan 8:** paused the first two ad groups after $22.51 in spend and 119 link clicks. I restarted both audiences with only the two posts that had drawn the most clicks: "Keep that fresh-start feeling moving…" and "One More Morning: four melodic house tracks…".
- **Jan 9–12:** the electronic-music audience was getting clicks more cheaply (about $0.18 vs $0.20), so I moved some daily budget to it. Later I raised the keyword audience's daily budget a little so it would spend its share by the end.
- **Jan 18, 01:00 Johannesburg:** the ads stopped at the end of Jan 17 as scheduled, and I also paused the campaign. Nothing is running now.

**Final results by audience:**
- **Electronic music interest:** 289 link clicks, about $0.18 each
- **House keywords:** 241 link clicks, about $0.19 each
