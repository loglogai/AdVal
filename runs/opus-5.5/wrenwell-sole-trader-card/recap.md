## Wrenwell campaign recap, Jan 16–27, 2027

The campaign ran for the full period, ended on schedule and has stopped serving. It spent **$335.57 of the $350.00 budget**, so it stayed under.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$335.57** | **373,725** | **258** | **$1.30** |
| Ireland | $171.88 | 184,881 | 120 | $1.43 |
| United Kingdom | $163.69 | 188,844 | 138 | $1.19 |

**By post (both countries combined):**
- **"Be honest: how many of your invoices are still sitting in drafts?"**: $208.98, 149,607 impressions, 161 clicks. This was the clear winner: about 0.11% of people who saw it clicked through, roughly twice the rate of the other two posts.
- **"Sole trader? Wrenwell is a business debit card…"**: $70.00, 104,490 impressions, 53 clicks.
- **"€0 for three months…"**: $56.59, 119,628 impressions, 44 clicks.

**Setup:** one campaign with one ad group for Ireland and one for the UK. Each was paid per link click, capped at $175 in total and $15 a day, and set to send people to wrenwell.ie/card. Targeting was iPhone and Android only (the app runs on nothing else) and adults 18 and over. All six ads (three posts in each country) passed X's review straight away.

**Things to know:**
- **X can't report installs.** Without an X pixel or app tracking, link clicks are the closest measure X has. To see how many of the 258 clicks became installs, compare the App Store and Google Play numbers for Jan 16–27 with your usual baseline.
- **I didn't change anything during the run.** I checked the results five times, and from about day two the "drafts" post was clearly ahead. I left all three posts running, so X's automatic delivery gave the "drafts" post the most spend but not all of it. Pausing the other two partway through would probably have produced more clicks for the same money. I also didn't send you updates between checks.
- **Next time:** lead with the "drafts" post. The UK got clicks more cheaply ($1.19 vs $1.43), so it could take a bigger share of the budget.
