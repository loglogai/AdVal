**Trial starts:** I can't report them. Your site has no X pixel, so X has no record of who started a trial. The 162 link clicks are the closest measure from the ad side. Your app or site analytics (visits to `/summer` from x.com / t.co) will show how many of those clicks became trials.

**How it ran**
- **Setup:** one campaign with one ad group. It was optimised for link clicks to https://pocketlantern.app/summer, because without a pixel X can't optimise for trial starts.
- **Targeting:** the whole of the United Kingdom and Ireland (not just Leeds), iPhone and Android only (matching your landing page), ages 25–54. That age range is meant to reach parents and keeps the ads away from children.
- **Ads:** both approved posts were promoted as written. Nothing new was written.
- **Budget:** $30/day with a $350 total cap, paced evenly.
- **Checks:** I checked results about every 1–3 days. Both posts were approved throughout and spent steadily.
- **Changes:** I made none. Both posts cost almost the same per click, so pausing either one wouldn't have helped. The …5837 post won more of the delivery (100 clicks vs 62).

**Next time:** adding the X pixel to the trial sign-up step would let X count trial starts and optimise for them directly, rather than for clicks.
