## Setup and changes
- **Structure:** one campaign with two ad groups, optimized for link clicks. One targeted the Nashville metro and the other the rest of the US. Both were limited to ages 18+ to match your petition rule. Political campaigning ads may only use certain targeting types, and location and age are both allowed. X approved all three of your posts in both ad groups, and I didn't write any new ones.
- **Budget changes:** Nashville clicks cost more than clicks elsewhere in the US, so I moved money away from it twice (June 8 and June 10). The Nashville ad group's total cap went from $250 to $145, and the rest of the US went from $450 to $555. The combined total cap stayed at $700 throughout.
