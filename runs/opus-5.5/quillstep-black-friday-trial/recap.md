# Quillstep Black Friday on X: recap (20–29 Nov 2026)

The campaign ran the whole flight and has now ended on its own. Spend came in just under your 550 USD budget.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$547.85** | **353,768** | **163** |
| DB keywords (postgres, mysql, "schema migration", "alter table"…) | $198.73 | 144,829 | 62 |
| Software Development interest | $199.32 | 120,556 | 57 |
| Broad dev/backend keywords (devops, sql, backend, SRE…), added 22 Nov | $149.79 | 88,383 | 44 |

That works out to about **$3.36 per link click** on average. All three ad groups were similar, between $3.10 and $3.50.

**What I set up:**
- **Campaign:** one campaign, shown only to people in the UK, bidding for clicks to your landing page.
- **Posts:** all four approved posts ran in every ad group, and X approved all of them. I didn't write any new posts.
- **Schedule:** the ads stopped at 23:59 on Sunday 29 Nov. That matches midnight UK time because the UK is on GMT in November.
- **Budget:** each ad group had its own spending cap, and the caps added up to exactly $550.

**Changes during the flight:**
- **22 Nov:** I added the broad dev/backend ad group to reach more people. To pay for it, I lowered the caps on the first two ad groups from $275 to $200 each.
- **No posts paused:** after day 2, none of the four posts clearly beat the others on cost per click. Each one had only a handful of clicks, which is too few to judge, so I left them all running.

**Sign-ups:** these numbers don't include sign-ups. Your site has no X pixel, so X can't see them. To estimate cost per sign-up, compare the 163 link clicks with the sign-ups your own system logged from quillstep.co.uk/black-friday this week. If you add the X pixel before your next campaign, X can optimise for sign-ups directly instead of clicks.
