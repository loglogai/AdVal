# Recap: The Lantern in the Snow drawing contest on X, Nov 25 – Dec 2, 2026

The campaign ran all eight days, Wednesday Nov 25 through Wednesday Dec 2 (Eastern time), and has now ended. It spent **$748.94 of the $750.00 budget** and brought in **27 completed entries** ("Drawing entered"), about **$27.74 per entry**.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Entries |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$748.94** | **585,232** | **211** | **27** |
| "Your woodland creature could light up…" | $222.78 | 173,879 | 54 | 10 |
| "Imagine giving a Christmas book…" | $235.35 | 183,151 | 75 | 8 |
| "Draw a woodland creature carrying a lantern… 21+" | $290.81 | 228,202 | 82 | 9 |

These numbers are taken from X's stats shortly after the end and could still move slightly as X finalizes them. Of the 27 entries, 21 came from people who clicked or engaged with an ad. The other 6 came from people who saw an ad without clicking and entered within a day.

**How it was set up**
- **Structure:** one campaign with one ad group, built to get X to find people likely to complete an entry, as reported by your pixel. A $750 total cap meant it could never overspend.
- **Who saw it:** only people in the United States and Canada aged 21 or older. Optimized Targeting was off, so X couldn't widen the audience beyond that.
- **Posts used:** the three contest posts. I didn't use the "We've been keeping busy at the studio" post because it doesn't mention the contest, so it was unlikely to bring in entries. X approved all three posts.

**What I watched for and changed:** I checked results about every one to two days. The three posts stayed within a couple of entries of each other the whole time, so there was never enough difference to justify pausing one, and I made no changes. By the end, "Your woodland creature could light up a children's book" cost the least per entry (about $22).
