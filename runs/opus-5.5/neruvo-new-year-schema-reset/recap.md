# Neruvo X campaign recap: Jan 4–6, 2027 (Accra time)

The campaign ran Monday 00:00 through Wednesday 24:00 Accra time and has now ended on its own. Total spend was **$2,623.95** of the $2,700 budget, so **$76.05 was left unspent**.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$2,623.95** | **2,348,276** | **978** | **$2.68** |
| GraphQL/API keywords ad group | $1,581.87 | 1,610,083 | 657 | $2.41 |
| Software-development interest ad group | $1,042.08 | 738,193 | 321 | $3.25 |

**I can't tell you how many sign-ups this produced.** Your site doesn't have X's pixel, so X never sees a sign-up. The campaign was set up to get as many clicks to neruvo.io/start as possible. Your own sign-up records for Jan 4–6 are the real measure: compare them with the 978 clicks.

**How the posts did** (both ad groups combined):
- **Post …5225** ("January 6 is the last day…"): 371 clicks at $2.65 each. Best performer.
- **Post …2585** ("New Year reset"): 299 clicks at $2.64 each.
- **Post …9496** ("30 days free"): 162 clicks at $2.70 each.
- **Post …5589** ("Developers and teams worldwide"): 146 clicks at $2.84 each.
- **Post …0299** ("less digital clutter"): I didn't run this one. It never mentions GraphQL or what Neruvo does, so it would likely draw clicks from people who won't sign up.

**What I changed along the way:**
- **Day 1:** Keyword clicks were cheaper, so I moved money from the interest ad group to the keyword ad group, adjusting the daily and total limits on each.
- **Day 1, afternoon:** I added more GraphQL keywords: Apollo, Hasura, federation, API versioning and GraphQL API.
- **Day 3:** I gave the keyword ad group a little more money. Even so, it didn't spend quite all of it, which is where the $76 comes from.

**Who saw the ads:** English-speaking X users in 13 countries: the US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, India, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Singapore and the Philippines. All five approved posts are in English, so I limited the ads to English speakers. That means this was English-speaking markets, not the whole world. Next time, if you approve your posts in other languages, the ads can reach the rest of your markets. Adding X's pixel would also let the campaign aim for sign-ups instead of clicks.
