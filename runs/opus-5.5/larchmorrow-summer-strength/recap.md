# Larchmorrow Strength X campaign recap: July 11–15, 2027

The campaign ran from Sunday, July 11 at 00:00 to Thursday, July 15 at 23:59 Mountain time. It has now ended on schedule and isn't spending anything.

## Totals for the flight

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Post A ("A little strength can fit into a summer day…") | $209.24 | 172,501 | 95 | $2.20 |
| Post B ("Your summer calendar has apparently forgotten…") | $286.17 | 237,378 | 148 | $1.93 |
| **Total** | **$495.41** | **409,879** | **243** | **$2.04** |

Total spend stayed under the $500.00 budget.

## What these numbers show and what they don't
- **Impressions** count how many times the ads appeared on screen.
- **Link clicks** count taps on the larchmorrow.com/start link. They don't count trial starts. X can't see what happens after someone reaches your site, so it can't tell whether they downloaded the app or activated the 14-day trial.
- To judge the result, compare your in-app trial-start count for July 11–15 against these 243 clicks and $495.41. Trial starts ÷ $495.41 gives your cost per trial. Some starts may come a few days after the click, so a slightly longer window may be fairer.

## How it was set up
- **Structure:** one campaign with one ad group per approved post. I didn't write any new copy. Each ad group was set to get as many link clicks as possible.
- **Locations:** the United States and Canada.
- **Devices:** only people using X on iPhone (iOS) or Android phones. Desktop, tablet and other devices were left out, since a phone is required for the trial.
- **Age:** no age limit, since you have no age requirement. I also didn't add any interest targeting.

## Changes during the flight
- **July 13:** I moved budget toward Post B. Over the first two days it was getting clicks at a lower cost, and it kept that lead.
- **July 14:** I moved more budget to Post B and raised its cap. The two caps still added up to $500.
- Both posts were approved right away and kept serving throughout.

## Suggestions for next time
- Post B's lighter, playful tone got more clicks per dollar. It's worth checking whether its clicks also led to more trials in your app reporting.
- If you want X to measure trial starts directly, you'd need an app tracking partner (often called an attribution SDK) connected to X. With that in place, a future campaign could be set to get as many trial starts as possible, not just clicks.
