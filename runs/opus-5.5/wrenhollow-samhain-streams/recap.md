# Samhain Night Songs: X campaign recap (23–31 Oct 2027, Dublin time)

The campaign ran for all nine days and ended on schedule. It is now expired and spending nothing.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broad audience, Ireland + UK | $418.28 | 336,438 | 204 | $2.05 |
| Music-interest audience, Ireland + UK (paused 25 Oct) | $30.67 | 18,252 | 10 | $3.07 |
| **Total** | **$448.95** | **354,690** | **214** | **$2.10** |

We spent $448.95 of the $450.00 budget.

**How it ran**
- **Measurement:** X can't see streams, so I optimised for link clicks to the landing page. That's the closest thing to a stream X can count. The 214 clicks are visits to the landing page, not confirmed plays. Your streaming services' stats for the same dates will show what actually came through.
- **Setup:** I used one campaign aimed only at Ireland and the UK, since that's where the album is free to stream. It had two ad groups: a broad audience and a music-interest audience.
- **Audience change:** The music-interest audience cost about twice as much per click and reached fewer people. I cut its budget on day 2, paused it on day 3, and moved its money to the broad audience.
- **Posts:** I ran four of your posts in both ad groups. The "Free. That's the price…" post got the most clicks, and the "plastic pumpkins" post came second. The two longer descriptive posts did a bit worse, but none was clearly weak enough to pause.
- **Post not used:** I left out "Our new album is out now…" because it doesn't say what the album is, who it's for, or that it's free.
- **Final days:** On 30 October I raised the daily cap so the money left would be spent during Halloween weekend. The total cap held spend under $450.
