# Cevorel "Two Together" X campaign recap, Feb 10–14, 2027 (Auckland time)

The campaign ran for the whole flight, from 00:00 on February 10 to the end of February 14, and finished when the gift-pack offer closed. Total spend was **$444.98 of the $450.00 budget**. It ran as one campaign with two ad groups, both shown only on iOS, worldwide except Russia and Belarus. Both ad groups were set to get as many link clicks to https://cevorel.app/together as possible.

## Campaign activity

These are X's figures for activity on X. They are not installs.

| Ad group | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Valentine gift post (…1605) | $253.54 | 623,694 | 433 | $0.59 |
| "What's singing outside your window?" (…0063) | $191.44 | 445,422 | 280 | $0.68 |
| **Total** | **$444.98** | **1,069,116** | **713** | **$0.62** |

## What I changed along the way
- **Feb 10:** Both posts started with equal budgets. They cost about the same per click on the first day.
- **Feb 11–13:** From day 2 the Valentine gift post got cheaper per click and stayed that way. The other post got more expensive by day 4 (about $0.72 per click). Each day I moved budget toward the gift post. By February 14 it had about $68 a day and the other post about $34. The two ad groups' total spending caps always added up to $450.
- **After the flight:** X had already stopped the campaign at its end time. I also set it to paused so it can't restart by mistake.

## Notes
- **The 713 link clicks are not installs.** They are taps through to your landing page. X can't see whether anyone installed from the App Store, so your own count of first-time installs is the result that matters. Comparing that count by day against the table above should show whether the gift post's cheaper clicks also brought more installs.
- I promoted only your two approved posts, and both are in English. Your localized versions weren't among the approved posts, so people outside English-speaking markets saw the English text.
