**What I did along the way**
- **Setup:** I used only your three approved posts and set every ad group to aim for as many "Album stream" pixel events as possible. Both ad groups targeted parents and families aged 25–54 everywhere except Russia and Belarus.
- **Budget shift:** On Saturday afternoon the Valentine's post was bringing in streams at about a third of the cost of the other two. "12 songs, 34 minutes" was the weakest of the three. I moved budget towards the Valentine's post: $60 for it and $40 for the other two.
- **After Valentine's Day:** At the end of Sunday I stopped the Valentine's post, because its "Valentine's Day is Sunday" wording would be out of date. The other ad group ran on through Monday. I adjusted its limit so total spend stayed under $100.

**One mistake on my part:** both ad groups only showed ads to people who use X in English. Your album page, landing page and posts are available in every major language, so that cut out most of the world. Next time, I'd drop the language filter and let each market see its own language version, which should reach far more people for the same money.
