## Recap: Last Light on the Towpath, 19–30 July 2027

The campaign ran all 12 days in UK time and spent **$348.94 of the $350.00 budget**. It brought in **104 streams**, about $3.36 per stream. I paused the campaign after it ended so nothing else can spend.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Streams |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "A song for the long evenings" (…962828) | $214.17 | 207,532 | 134 | 65 |
| "Stream free in your browser" (…350526) | $134.77 | 128,835 | 73 | 39 |
| **Total** | **$348.94** | **336,367** | **207** | **104** |

**How it was set up:** one campaign with one ad group for each of your two approved posts. Each ad group targeted the UK and Ireland, ages 13 and up (X doesn't allow ads to reach anyone under 13). I set them to optimise for your "Release opened" pixel event so the delivery system would aim for streams. Both posts were approved and served the whole time.

**What I changed:** I started with an even split between the two posts. After three days the "long evenings" post was getting more link clicks for the same spend, so I moved budget toward it (up to $215 total, with $135 left on the other post). It stayed ahead for the rest of the flight.

**Something to know:** the stream count is what your pixel credited to the ads. 94 of the streams came after someone clicked an ad and 10 came from people who only saw an ad. If someone opened the player more than once, each opening is counted, which matches how you count streams. The count doesn't include plays from people who reached your site without going through an ad.
