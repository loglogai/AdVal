I ran the campaign for the full flight, Sept 5 through Sept 12 Toronto time, and it has now ended on schedule. Spend was **$818.73 of the $850.00 budget**. X can't see your bookings, so **I have no figure for confirmed demos**. The number that answers whether this worked has to come from your scheduling system.

## Setup
- **Campaign:** "Aisle Rehearsal – Back-to-School Demo Bookings – Sep 5–12 2027". It ran from 00:00 Sept 5 to 23:59 Sept 12 Toronto time.
- **Two ad groups:** both targeted the United States and Canada, sent people to keldracksafety.com/demo and were optimized for link clicks. Without a pixel, X can't optimize for bookings, so link clicks were the closest goal available. Each ad group had $425 total and about $53/day, paced evenly.
  - **Safety & training:** keywords such as warehouse safety, forklift safety, OSHA, WHMIS, loading dock safety and EHS manager.
  - **Warehouse operations & logistics:** keywords such as warehouse operations and manager, distribution or fulfillment center, 3PL, employee onboarding and shift supervisor.
- **Ads:** all five approved posts ran in both ad groups, and all were approved by X. I didn't write any new posts.

## Results reported by X (Sept 5–12)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safety & training | $411.88 | 256,360 | 117 | $3.52 |

| **Total** | **$818.73** | **602,747** | **225** | **$3.64** |

These are clicks through to the demo page, not bookings. A click doesn't tell us whether someone opened the calendar, let alone confirmed an appointment.

## How I managed it
I checked delivery several times during the flight. **I didn't change anything**: both ad groups spent steadily, and their costs per click were close. By the halfway point, two posts were clearly getting the most clicks:
- **"Does your September reset need a reset?…"** (2227270321569957229)
- **"Bring warehouse learning back into the routine…"** (2226545545835828797)

Two posts got very few clicks:
- **"Free 20-minute demo…"** (2227089127635860770): 4 clicks across both ad groups by Sept 9
- **"Warehouse employers in the United States and Canada…"** (2226726739770895417): 7 clicks by Sept 9 in the operations ad group

Click counts that low aren't enough to show a post leads to fewer bookings, so I left all five running. Pausing the two weak posts on Sept 9 was a reasonable option I didn't take. I left about $31 unspent because both ad groups had caps at their share of the budget.

## Confirmed demo bookings
**Not available to me.** To judge the campaign on bookings:
- Count the appointments your scheduling system confirmed between **Sept 5 and Sept 12 (Toronto time)**.
- Compare that with your usual weekly baseline. Any lift is only a rough estimate of what the campaign added, because X didn't track individual visitors and the posts all used the same untagged link.
- Divide **$818.73** by that number to get your cost per booked demo.

For future campaigns, adding a separate tracking tag to the link for each post or ad group, or installing X's pixel to record a confirmed booking, would let us link bookings directly to these ads.
