# Selnivo Flow campaign recap: Oct 20–27, 2027 (Manila time)

The campaign ran all eight days and has now ended on its own. It spent **$941.60 of the $950 budget**. I can't tell you how many free trials it produced. The site has no X pixel, so X never saw a trial start. Link clicks are the closest result I can measure, so I optimized for them.

| Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Has your chair seen more of you…" | $562.87 | 1,357,847 | 934 | $0.60 |
| "Make movement part of your workday…" | $273.40 | 738,281 | 377 | $0.73 |
| "Guided shoulder and hip mobility…" (paused after day 3) | $105.33 | 224,456 | 123 | $0.86 |
| **Total** | **$941.60** | **2,320,584** | **1,434** | **$0.66** |

**What I did:** I set up one campaign with one ad group per post. Each ad group was shown only on iPhone and Android phones, worldwide, to people using X in English. Each post ran for three days on an equal budget. After that I paused "Shoulder and hip," the most expensive per click, moved most of the budget to "Chair," the cheapest, and kept "Make movement" running at a lower level. "Chair" stayed at about $0.60 a click even after its budget roughly doubled.

**What the results show:**
- **Which post wins on clicks:** "Chair" got about 18% cheaper clicks than "Make movement" and about 30% cheaper than "Shoulder and hip."
- **What they don't show:** whether those clicks became trials, or which post brought the best sign-ups. To get trial counts, compare 1,434 clicks with your own trial starts from X traffic for Oct 20–27. Some clicks will be lost before the page loads, so expect fewer visits than clicks.
- **Limited reach:** all three approved posts are in English, so only people using X in English saw them. The versions in other languages weren't among the approved posts, so none of them ran.

**Next time:** install X's pixel on the site and track trial starts as a conversion. Then X can optimize for trials, and the results will report them directly.
