# 🎃 Lanternfox Halloween recap (Oct 27–31, 2027, Eastern)

The campaign ran the full five days and stopped on schedule at midnight Eastern after Halloween. Spend stayed within your $550 budget.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$549.28** | **449,224** | **254** | **$2.16** |
| Ad group A: "Ghost stories hit different…" and the "phone app" explainer | $165.48 | 176,713 | 66 | $2.51 |
| Ad group B: "$0 for 14 days" and "Halloween is Sunday" | $383.79 | 272,511 | 188 | $2.04 |

**By post:**
- **"Halloween is Sunday 🎃":** $232.02 spent, 156,205 impressions, 115 clicks. This was the best post; it brought in 45% of all clicks.
- **"$0 for 14 days":** $151.78 spent, 116,306 impressions, 73 clicks
- **"Ghost stories hit different":** $106.96 spent, 101,785 impressions, 43 clicks
- **Explainer ("Lanternfox is a phone app…"):** $58.52 spent, 74,928 impressions, 23 clicks. This was the weakest post.

**How I set it up:**
- **Campaign:** one campaign optimized for link clicks. Without the X pixel, X can't see trial starts, so clicks to lanternfox.app/halloween were the closest thing I could optimize for.
- **Targeting:** both ad groups showed ads only to people in the United States on iPhone or Android, so no money went to people who couldn't start a trial.
- **Posts:** only your 4 approved posts ran, and all 4 passed X's review right away.

**Changes I made along the way:**
- **Moving budget:** the free trial and Halloween urgency posts got clicks for less from day one. I moved budget toward them after day 1 and again after day 3.
- **Pausing ad group A:** going into Halloween day, I paused A so the last day's budget went entirely to B. On Halloween, B got 41 clicks, its best day.

**What to check:** these numbers are clicks, not trial starts. Please compare them with the trial starts you tracked from Oct 27–31 to get your real cost per trial. For next year, putting X's pixel on the trial confirmation page would let X optimize for trial starts directly. Short "it's free" and "Halloween is coming" messages clearly beat the longer explainer, which is worth remembering for future copy.

Happy haunting! 🦊👻
