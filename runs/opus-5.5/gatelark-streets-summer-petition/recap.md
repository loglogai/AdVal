## Gatelark Streets campaign recap (24 June – 4 July 2027)

The campaign ran for the full 11 days, from midnight BST on 24 June to midnight BST on 4 July, and stayed within budget. I've now paused it, so it can't spend any more.

| Metric | Total |
|---|---|
| **Spend** | **$446.50** of $450.00 |
| **Impressions** | **352,835** |
| **Link clicks** | **171** |
| Petition signatures (reported by your "Petition signed" pixel) | 21 (about $21 each) |

**Setup:**
- **Structure:** one campaign with one ad group. It was optimised for "Petition signed" conversions and capped at a $450 total budget, with spend spread evenly at up to about $41 a day.
- **Audience:** United Kingdom, aged 18 and over, matching your participation policy. Optimized Targeting was off, so the ads only reached that audience. All targeting fits X's rules for political campaigning ads.
- **Posts:** I promoted posts 2201102897773338130, 2201284091706019029 and 2201465285639200080, and X approved all three. I didn't use post 2201646479570704685 because it doesn't say that signers must be UK residents aged 18+. That would probably have brought in clicks from people who can't sign.
- **Monitoring:** I checked the campaign on days 1, 4, 8 and at the end. The three posts performed about the same, with post 2201465285639200080 slightly ahead. With this few signatures the gap wasn't big enough to justify moving budget, so I made no changes during the flight.

About 1 in 8 people who clicked went on to sign. If you run another round, it's worth looking at the signing page to see if more visitors could be turned into signatures.
