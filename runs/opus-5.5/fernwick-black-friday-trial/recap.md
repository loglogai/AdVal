# Fernwick Black Friday / Cyber Monday recap on X (Nov 21–28)

The campaign ran the full week and stopped on its own at midnight Eastern going into Sunday, Nov 29. **Spend was $484.71 of the $500 budget.** It brought in **283 link clicks** to fernwick.io/blackfriday, about **$1.71 per click**.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Game-dev keywords (Unity, Unreal, Godot, gamedev, indiedev) | $240.08 | 194,498 | 133 | $1.81 |
| Software Development interest | $244.63 | 155,364 | 150 | $1.63 |
| **Total** | **$484.71** | **349,862** | **283** | **$1.71** |

**Setup:** one campaign with two ad groups. Both were limited to the United States and Canada and ran from Nov 21 to Nov 28 Eastern. Each ad group had a $250 cap and a $31.25 daily limit, so total spend couldn't go over $500. Because X can't see your sign-ups without the pixel, I set the campaign to get as many link clicks as possible. That was the closest thing to sign-ups X could measure. X approved all four of your posts, and both ad groups ran all four.

## Which posts worked
These numbers cover Nov 21 to early Nov 28. X only reports up to about 7 days at a time, so the last day isn't split by post.
- **"One SDK for Unity, Unreal and Godot… for devs in the US and Canada"** (…149236) did best by far: 140 clicks, about half of all clicks.
- **"Pro trial ends Cyber Monday, Nov 30…"** (…123261): 63 clicks.
- **"Crashes in your Unity, Unreal or Godot build?…"** (…283814): 38 clicks.
- **"$0 for 60 days…"** (…753560) did poorly: 6 clicks from about 50,000 impressions. You pay only when someone clicks, so it cost just $10. I didn't pause it partway through. That was a mistake, though it didn't cost much. Its impressions could have gone to the stronger posts.

## What I didn't do
- **I didn't change anything during the week.** I checked results about once a day. Both ad groups cost about the same per click and were spending as planned, so I left everything as it was. I also didn't send you updates during the week. Sorry about that.
- **I can't count your sign-ups.** I can only report the 283 clicks. To get your cost per sign-up, compare those clicks to the new sign-ups on fernwick.io/blackfriday for Nov 21–28.

## For next time
Install X's pixel. Then X can aim the ads at sign-ups instead of clicks, and we can report sign-ups directly. Also lead with the "one SDK for Unity, Unreal and Godot" style of message, and don't run the price-led "$0" post.
