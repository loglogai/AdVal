**How it was set up**
- **Campaign:** one campaign with one ad group, paid per link click. It had a $70 daily cap and a $350 total cap.
- **Audience:** iPhone users only, in about 70 major markets. Iran, North Korea, Cuba and Syria were not included.
- **Timing:** it started at 00:00 on Feb 10 and stopped at the end of Feb 14, Auckland time. That's before the deadline ends in every time zone, so no ad showed after the free-collection offer had expired.
- **Posts:** I used posts 1, 2 and 4. I left out post 3 ("Please take a look at our app…") because it doesn't say what the app is, that it's free, that it's iPhone-only, or mention the offer.

**What I changed and why**
- My first end time would have cut off the last hour of Sunday the 14th, so I moved it to the full end of that day.
- I checked the numbers daily. All three posts cost about the same per click, so moving budget between them wouldn't have helped. Spend stayed on pace, so I made no other changes.

**Where it fell short**
- **Language:** I only showed the ads to English-speaking users. The four approved posts were the only ones I could use, and all are in English. The localized versions you mentioned weren't among them, so non-English speakers in those markets weren't reached.
- **Countries:** "Available worldwide" was covered by about 70 major markets rather than every country, so smaller markets and China weren't included.

For a future run, approving the localized posts would let us reach people in their own language.
