# Couch Captain recap (Feb 11–15, 2027, Arizona time)

The campaign ran all five days and has now ended on schedule. It spent $649.64 of your $650.00 budget.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | "Photo entered" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ad group 1: all 4 posts (Feb 11–12) | $260.00 | 196,323 | 89 | 13 |
| Ad group 2: posts 2–4 (Feb 13–15) | $389.64 | 305,035 | 133 | 15 |
| **Total** | **$649.64** | **501,358** | **222** | **28** |

- **Cost:** about $23 per contest entry and $2.93 per link click.
- **How entries were counted:** 22 of the 28 entries came from people who clicked or engaged with an ad. The other 6 are "view-through": people saw an ad without clicking and entered within a day. Your site's own entry count may differ slightly.

**Setup:** I ran one campaign that adjusts bids automatically to get as many "Photo entered" conversions as possible. It sent people to xamblepawphotos.com/couch-captain and was shown only to people in the United States aged 21 and over, to match your contest rules. All four of your approved posts passed X's review, and I didn't write any new ones.

**One change during the flight:** After two days, post 1 ("Your pet's couch pose deserves an audience…") had produced 1 entry for $59 in spend. Each of the other three had about 4. On Feb 13 I stopped the first ad group and put the remaining $390 into a new one with posts 2, 3 and 4. I capped each ad group's budget so the two together couldn't go over $650.

**Which posts worked best:**
- **Post 2** ("You bought the couch. Your pet inherited the entire estate…") was the strongest overall, with 11 entries.
- **Post 3** (the deadline reminder) had the most link clicks early on, but brought fewer entries in the last few days.
- **Post 4** (the 21+ eligibility post) was the weakest at the end.

The humorous post did best, which is worth remembering for your next contest. I hope the entries include some excellent couch captains.
