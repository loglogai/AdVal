## Setup
- **Structure:** one campaign with two ad groups, so the invitation and the trial for yourself are reported separately. Both ad groups were set to get as many link clicks as possible. They used only your 4 approved posts.
- **Audience:** both groups were limited to Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, and to iPhone and Android devices. They were also aimed at people interested in anime or horror and thriller. X doesn't let me target phones only by excluding tablets, so a small share of the impressions may have gone to iPads or Android tablets.

## Changes during the flight
- **Only one adjustment:** on December 16, I raised the daily budget for the trial-for-yourself group from $22 to $28. It was spending below pace, and that group had the most clicks.
- **No ads paused:** the cost per click was about $2.04–$2.30 for all 4 posts. The differences between them were in how often people clicked, not in price. So I didn't pause any.
- **After the flight:** I set the campaign to paused so it can't restart.
- **Unspent budget:** about $26 went unspent, because the ad groups didn't use all of their daily budgets.
