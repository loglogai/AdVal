# Thimblepot New Year push: final recap

The campaign ran from Saturday 2 January to the end of Tuesday 12 January, UK time, and has now finished. It spent **$444.69** of the $450.00 budget, so nothing went over.

| | Post A: "New year, new pots" | Post B: "Resolution wobbled" | **Total** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spend | $285.98 | $158.70 | **$444.69** |
| Impressions | 272,161 | 159,760 | **431,921** |
| Link clicks | 135 | 66 | **201** |
| Cost per link click | $2.12 | $2.40 | $2.21 |

**Installs:** X can't see installs because there's no pixel on the site and no app tracking. Link clicks to thimblepot.co.uk/new-year are the closest thing X can measure. To get the real install number, compare your own app store or analytics figures for 2–12 January against these 201 clicks.

**What I set up and changed**
- **Setup:** one campaign with one ad group for each of your two approved posts. Both ad groups were set to get as many link clicks as possible, and both targeted the UK and people aged 18+ only, to match the landing page's eligibility rules and your financial services approval. I didn't write any new posts. X approved both ads, and they stayed approved for the whole run.
- **5 January:** Post A was bringing in more clicks for each dollar, so I moved budget towards it. Its daily cap went up from $20 to $25 and Post B's went down from $20 to $15.
- **11 January:** Post A was still ahead, so I raised its daily cap to $45 for the final push. I also cut Post B's total budget so the two together stayed under $450.

**Takeaway:** the direct offer post, with "free to download, Family Plus free for 3 months", beat the resolution post. It got clicks about 12% cheaper and earned twice as many in total. I'd lead with that post next time.
