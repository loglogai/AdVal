**How it was set up**
- **Structure:** one campaign with one ad group per approved post, and no new posts. Both ads were approved when they were attached.
- **Targeting:** United States, ages 18 and up, which matches your eligibility policy and uses only targeting types allowed for political campaigning ads. Audience expansion was off so ads stayed inside that audience.
- **Optimization:** for link clicks. Without X's pixel, X can't count sign-ups, so link clicks are the closest thing it can optimize for.
- **Budget:** each ad group had a spending cap, and the two caps added up to exactly $1,000.

**What I changed:** On July 19, Post A ("Summer errands need a restroom stop") was getting clicks more cheaply. I moved $40 of the remaining budget to it, so the caps went from $500 each to $540 for Post A and $460 for Post B. Post B got more impressions and likes but fewer clicks.

**What I can't tell you:** how many people actually signed. Please check the completed signatures on your own site for July 16–20. If you run something like this again, adding X's pixel to the petition's confirmation page would let X optimize for real sign-ups.
