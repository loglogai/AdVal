# Brackenhold back-to-school recap: 30 July – 10 August 2027

The campaign ran for the full flight, from 30 July to the end of 10 August on Dublin time, and has now ended on schedule. It spent **$391.07 of the $400 budget**.

## Headline numbers
| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total** | **$391.07** | **350,704** | **216** | **$1.81** |
| Climbing keywords (climbing, bouldering, hangboard, fingerboard, "climbing wall", "climbing club") | $212.74 | 196,531 | 109 | $1.95 |
| Fitness/Workouts interest | $178.33 | 154,173 | 107 | $1.67 |

## What I set up
- **One campaign with two ad groups.** Both were optimised for link clicks to brackenhold.ie/trial, shown only in Ireland and the UK and only on iPhone and Android.
- **All four approved posts ran in both ad groups.** X accepted all of them, and I didn't write any new posts.
- **Mid-flight change (4 Aug):** the fitness ad group was getting clicks more cheaply, so I moved $20 of the remaining budget from the keyword ad group to it.

## How the posts did (both ad groups combined)
| Post | Link clicks | Spend | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Term starts in a few weeks…" (…7849) | 66 | $117.38 | $1.78 |
| "Back on the wall this term?…" (…2322) | 55 | $94.90 | $1.73 |
| Pricing: "Free for 14 days, then €8.99…" (…4310) | 55 | $107.06 | $1.95 |
| "Plans your climbing training week by week…" (…3528) | 40 | $71.72 | $1.79 |

The two back-to-school posts brought in the most clicks. The post about the plan and hang timer got the most impressions but the fewest clicks for its reach. The pricing post's clicks cost the most. Still, all four were within about 20% of each other on cost per click, so I kept them all running rather than pausing any.
