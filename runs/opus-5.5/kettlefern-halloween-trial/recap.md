## Kettlefern Lantern Evenings Halloween recap (Oct 22–31, 2027)

The campaign ran all 10 days and has now ended on its own.

| | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Cost per click |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: Anime interest, UK/IE 18+ | $247.71 | 185,753 | 92 | $2.69 |
| B: Anime keywords, UK/IE 18+ | $245.75 | 119,384 | 98 | $2.51 |
| **Total** | **$493.47** | **305,137** | **190** | **$2.60** |

Total spend stayed within the $500 budget. Each ad group had a $250 cap, so the two together could never go over $500.

**Free-trial starts:** I couldn't report these because your site doesn't have X's pixel, so X never saw a trial start. To measure the real result, check your own sign-up data for Oct 22–31 from people who came via X to /lantern-evenings. All 190 clicks went to that page.

**How it was set up**
- One campaign with two ad groups. Both were set to get as many link clicks as possible, since that was the closest goal X could track. We only paid when someone clicked the link.
- Targeting was the UK and Ireland, ages 18+, to match who can actually sign up. Group A reached people X lists as interested in anime. Group B reached people using words like anime, iyashikei, slice of life, Crunchyroll, Studio Ghibli and cozy anime.
- Ads ran from midnight UK time on Oct 22 to midnight UK time on Nov 1. That accounts for the clocks going back on Oct 31.

**What I learned and changed**
- I ran the two posts that mention the free trial and price, 2243139890184686641 ("Gentle small-town stories…") and 2243683471980145632 ("7-day free trial, then £4.99/month…"). I left out the other two because they don't mention the trial, so they would probably bring in more casual clicks.
- The "Gentle small-town stories" post got most of the clicks: 105 of the first 138, and it stayed ahead to the end. The "7-day free trial" post was shown a lot but got far fewer clicks. I kept it running because we only paid when someone clicked, so it cost little. Its clicks also came from people who had already seen the price and age rules, so they're probably more likely to start a trial.
- Keywords (Group B) got about twice as many clicks per view as the anime interest group (Group A). The cost per click was similar.
- On Oct 31 I raised the daily limits so both groups could use the rest of their budget on Halloween itself.

**Room to improve:** I checked results every day or two, not daily, and made only one change. Next time, it would help more to cut back or pause the "7-day free trial" post earlier and give more budget to the keyword group.

**For next time:** adding X's pixel to the sign-up confirmation page would let X aim the ads at people who actually start trials, and let us report trial starts directly.
