### Key Optimizations & Learnings
1. **Shifting Budget to UK-Wide Parents, Educators & Readers**: While London-specific targeting (`6iygj`, `bg398`, `o4yct`) drove steady clicks at ~$2.85–$3.10 CPC, expanding to 18+ UK residents interested in **Education, Parenting, Family, Career, and Politics** (`gm9d4`) and high-intent **library/reading/study keywords** (`nvx9x`) unlocked much lower auction costs (**$1.99 CPC** on `gm9d4` and **$2.42 CPC** on `nvx9x`).
2. **Pruning Low-Intent Lookalikes & Broad Keywords Early**: The follower lookalike ad group (`pktpa`) was paused after Day 3 once it showed a high CPC ($4.23/click), and overly broad single-word keywords (`book`, `student`, `exams`, `WFH`) were tested and removed in favor of library- and reading-focused terms (`library`, `libraries`, `public library`, `local library`, `reading`, `books`, `read more`, `revising`, `studying`, `Lewisham`).
3. **Riding the New Year's Resolution Wave**: Reallocating budget into the top two UK-wide ad groups during the final week (Dec 25–31) increased weekly link clicks by **64%** (from 70 in Week 1 to 115 in Week 2) while dropping average CPC from **$2.84** to **$2.16**.
