### Key Optimizations & Learnings Across the 4 Days
1. **Day 1 Testing & Creative Winner Isolation:** On Day 1, we tested all 5 approved posts across three audience segments (Calisthenics High-Intent `dxrbg`, Back-to-School Student 18–24 `evs1a`, and Broad Fitness `181gp`). Three posts clearly stood out: **`2215492715935231649`** (*"Zero is a perfectly good starting number"*), **`2215130328072545000`** (*"14 days free. That's the whole pitch... no gym membership needed"*), and **`2215311522002850276`** (*"Classes start soon, and so does the 14-day free trial"*), delivering nearly **3x higher CTR** than the two longer progression-list posts (`2214949134138782848` and `2215673909871009287`).
2. **Days 2–4 Budget Reallocation:**
   * After Day 1, we paused the broad interest group (`181gp`) and launched **`vod4n` (LI4)** targeting 18–34 iOS users in the US & Canada with **only the top 3 creatives**, plus increased daily budget on our best-performing student ad group (**`evs1a`**).
   * After Day 2, when impression dilution from the two slower posts lowered CTR in `dxrbg` (LI1), we paused `dxrbg` and shifted its budget into **`sblfo` (LI5)** (promoting only the top 2 posts to 18–24 calisthenics/student audiences) and further scaled **`evs1a`** and **`vod4n`** through the August 7 deadline.
