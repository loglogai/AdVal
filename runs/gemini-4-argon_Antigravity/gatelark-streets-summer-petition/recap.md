### What Worked & How We Managed the Flight

1. **Initial Testing (Days 1–3):** We launched all four approved posts across three distinct audience segments: Parents/Family/Education interests (`lo0jo`), Road Safety & School Streets keywords (`856i5`), and Civic/Active Travel + Follower Lookalikes (`huufz`).
2. **Creative Optimization (Days 4–5):** Early data showed that **Post `2201646479570704685`** (*"School-gate chatter, sunshine, and a walk that feels calmer. Too much to ask? Help us change the pace..."*) generated more than double the click-through rate and the lowest cost per signature compared to the more formal petition announcements, with **Post `2201102897773338130`** in second place. We paused the initial ad groups and focused budget on those top two creatives (`wlwpb` and `8470y`).
3. **Scaling the Winner (Days 6–11):** By Day 5, Post `2201646479570704685` paired with high-intent keyword targeting (`school streets`, `20mph`, `road safety`, `school run`, `walk to school`, `safe streets`, `speed limit`) proved to be the clear standout performer. We isolated Post `2201646479570704685` in `st5zn` and progressively shifted budget into it through the final day of the petition, delivering 15 signatures and 135 link clicks in that line item alone while exhausting the exact $450.00 budget at the close of Sunday, 4 July. All campaign entities are now paused.
