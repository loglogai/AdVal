### Key Optimizations Made During the Run
1. **Early Creative Pruning (Day 1 & Day 2):** We initially tested all 5 approved posts in dedicated line items optimized for `LINK_CLICKS`. Post `2153162003052675173` (*"Our new soundtrack is out now..."*) had the highest cost per link click ($0.64) and CPM, so we paused LI5 after 6 hours. Post `2152799615188857662` had the lowest link CTR (0.043%), so we paused LI1 on Day 2 to focus budget on the top performers.
2. **Scaling Winning Creatives:** Post `2153524390918485072` (the closing credits / cello & pelican hook) and Post `2153343196984500975` (the "$0 / Free on all major streaming services" hook) consistently delivered ~2x the link CTR of the other posts and drove **394 of the 439 total link clicks (89.7%)** at **$0.43–$0.45 CPC**.
3. **Audience & Expansion Refinement:** Switching `audience_expansion` from `EXPANDED` to `DEFINED`, removing overly broad `Movies & TV / Drama` targeting, and combining `@MarrowLaneScore` follower + follower-lookalike targeting with `Music`, `Streaming`, `Film Festivals`, and `Relationships`/`Valentine's` criteria substantially improved click efficiency heading into Valentine's weekend.
