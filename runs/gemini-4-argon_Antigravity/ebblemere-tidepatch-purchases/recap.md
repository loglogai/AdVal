### Key Optimization Decisions
1. **Buyer Pre-Qualification & Creative Selection**: Because X cannot track store conversions without a pixel, optimizing for link clicks alone risks paying for low-intent curiosity clicks. We excluded **Post `2180884674770473614`** (which omitted the game's title, genre, and $18 price) and promoted only **Posts `2181065868706016722` and `2180703480841134588`**, both of which clearly state the genre and the **US$18 one-time purchase** upfront.
2. **Eligibility & Audience Targeting**: All ad groups enforced **`AGE_OVER_18`** to match store purchase eligibility, served worldwide across all eligible markets with **`DEFINED`** audience expansion (no off-target expansion) on **`ALL_ON_TWITTER`** placements, and targeted **`INTEREST: Gaming`**. Early broad keyword and lookalike criteria on `xne5k` delivered less than half the CTR (`0.031%–0.042%`) of Gaming interest (`0.075%–0.085%`) and were removed after the first 8 hours, immediately doubling `xne5k`'s click rate.
3. **Budget Reallocation**: As **Post `2181065868706016722`** consistently outperformed **Post `2180703480841134588`** on both CTR and CPC ($1.56 vs. $1.65), daily budget was progressively shifted from `676cs` ($200 $\rightarrow$ $160 $\rightarrow$ $100 $\rightarrow$ $80/day) to `6zhva` ($200 $\rightarrow$ $250 $\rightarrow$ $315 $\rightarrow$ $325/day). All line items and the campaign are now paused.
