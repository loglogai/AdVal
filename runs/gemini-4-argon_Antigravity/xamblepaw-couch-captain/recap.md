### Key Optimization Decisions During the Flight
1. **Pruning Non-Converting Audience Segments (Day 1):** We initially tested three audience segments: Pet Interests (`Pets`, `Pets / Cats`, `Pets / Dogs`), Brand Followers/Lookalikes + Pet Keywords (`"my dog"`, `"my cat"`, `"pet photo"`, `"dog on couch"`, `"cat on couch"`), and broader Super Bowl NFL / Photography / Home interests (`z2p8x`). Mid-way through Day 1, `z2p8x` had delivered over 7,300 impressions with 0 link clicks and 0 entries, so we paused it and reallocated budget to the two pet-owner segments.
2. **Isolating Top-Converting Posts by Audience (Days 2–5):**
   * **Post `2152663719739489920`** generated 0 entries on ~$26 in initial testing, so we rotated away from it after Day 1.
   * **Post `2153026107602269511`** (*"Super Bowl sofa stars: your photo deadline is February 15, 2027!..."*) was the top overall performer across both audiences, driving **20 completed entries**.
   * **Post `2153207301534733117`** (*"United States pet owners aged 21+: enter Couch Captain free..."*) paired best with the **Pet Interests** audience (`zh15y`), contributing **8 completed entries** inside `zh15y` alone.
   * **Post `2152844913669137757`** (*"You bought the couch. Your pet inherited the entire estate..."*) drove strong click-through and conversion rates with **Brand Followers/Lookalikes & Pet Keywords** (`kuvzb`), delivering **8 completed entries** in `kuvzb`.
