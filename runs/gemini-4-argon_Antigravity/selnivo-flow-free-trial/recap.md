### What the Results Actually Show
1. **Link Clicks vs. Free-Trial Starts:** Because `selnivoflow.app/start` does not have an X conversion pixel or app event tracking installed, **X can only measure traffic sent to your landing page (1,226 link clicks), not how many visitors actually started a seven-day free trial.** To evaluate true cost-per-trial, compare your internal backend trial sign-ups from October 20–27 against the 1,226 link clicks and $940.95 spend.
2. **Audience Insights:** Users targeted via **Health & Fitness interests (`LI2`)** were the most responsive to clicking through (0.088% CTR, 543 link clicks), prompting us to scale their budget allocation from $320 up to ~$418 over the 8 days. **Keyword targeting (`LI3`)** achieved the lowest cost per click ($0.72), while **Desk & Tech interests (`LI1`)** provided steady volume across a broader reach.
3. **Creative Insights:** Post `2243758969454239118` (*"Has your chair seen more of you than your friends this week?"*) generated the strongest click-through rate (0.071%) and drove 42.6% of all link clicks, though note that its copy is less explicit about platform requirements (*iOS/Android phones only*) prior to the click than Post `2243577775521572387` (which we protected by restricting all ad groups strictly to iOS and Android devices at the targeting level).
