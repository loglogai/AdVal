### Thimblepot New Year Push — Campaign Recap

The New Year campaign (`buu8n`) ran from **Saturday, January 2, 2027 (00:00 UK time)** through **Tuesday, January 12, 2027 (23:59 UK time)** and is now paused and completed.

* **Total Spend:** **$445.32 USD** *(out of $450.00 budget — 99.0% utilization)*
* **Total Impressions:** **439,931**
* **Total Link Clicks:** **213** *(average CPC: $2.09)*

#### Key Optimizations Made During the Run
1. **Mobile-Only & Eligibility Filtering:** Restricted all delivery to iOS and Android users aged 18+ in the UK to ensure every click landed on a device capable of installing the app and met both UK financial services rules and your landing page's 18+ terms.
2. **Budget Reallocation to High-Intent & Low-CPC Segments:** Scaled budget into the **Personal Finance & Savings Keywords** ad group (which drove the highest click volume at 89 link clicks and $1.95 CPC) and the **UK 25–54 Mobile** demographic group as its CPC dropped to $1.65 in the second half of the campaign.
3. **Goal Testing:** Tested a `SITE_VISITS` optimization line item (`xg31e`) alongside `LINK_CLICKS`, then tapered and paused it over the final days once `LINK_CLICKS` proved significantly more cost-effective ($1.65–$2.40 CPC vs. $2.84 CPC).
