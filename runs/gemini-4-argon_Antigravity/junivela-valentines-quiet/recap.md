**Key Adjustments Made During the Flight:**
1. **Isolating Explicit iPhone App Copy (`apfhr`)**: Halfway through Day 1, we observed that Post `2152452326815646911` (*"A little Valentine freebie awaits"*) captured roughly 60% of clicks inside multi-post ad groups, whereas Posts `2152089938952500621` and `2152271132883638875` explicitly pre-qualified visitors by stating that Junivela is a **free iPhone sleep-sounds app** and that unlocking *Veranda for Two* requires **installing and opening the app by February 14** (preventing expectations of a physical gift or voucher code). We launched `apfhr` with only those two explicit-app posts to ensure high-intent, pre-qualified traffic alongside the main ad groups.
2. **Pausing Expensive Valentine/Relationship Auction Traffic (`xg6lj`)**: After Day 1, `xg6lj` showed a $0.660 CPC—nearly double the cost of our evening-routine and sleep segments. We paused `xg6lj` at the end of Day 1 and reallocated its budget to `jbigt`, `v1fyu`, and `apfhr`.
3. **Refining Interests on `apfhr` & Scaling Top Performers through Sunday**: At the end of Day 1, we removed the overly broad *Music* interest (`ire8uoy`) from `apfhr` and added *Home & Garden*, *Podcasts*, and the *reading* keyword, which dropped `apfhr`'s daily CPC from $0.607 on Day 1 down to $0.333–$0.376 on Days 2–4. We also scaled `jbigt` (our most cost-efficient ad group at $0.340 average CPC) up to $29/day through Valentine's Day Sunday.
