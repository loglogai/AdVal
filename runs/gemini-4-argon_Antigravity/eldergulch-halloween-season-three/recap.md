#### How We Managed It Through the Week
1. **Early Testing (Days 1–2):** We launched all three ad groups with all four approved posts to test where high-intent podcast listeners were clicking most efficiently.
2. **Pruning & Scaling (Day 2 Onward):** AG3 (Broad Lookalikes & Sci-Fi/Crime) showed a significantly higher cost per click ($2.97 CPC) and lower click-through rate, so we paused it after Day 2 and reallocated its remaining budget into **AG2 (Keywords)** and **AG1 (Horror & Podcasts Interests)**.
3. **Expanding High-Intent Keywords:** As AG2 emerged as our strongest driver of traffic ($1.37 CPC), we expanded its keyword list mid-week to capture seasonal search and conversation intent (*spooky podcast, halloween podcast, horror audio drama, paranormal podcast*) and steadily scaled its daily budget through Halloween night.
