### Key Optimizations & Learnings

1. **Compliance & Eligibility Filtering**:
   - All ad groups strictly targeted **Ireland and the United Kingdom** (matching your financial services authorization and product availability), **iOS and Android devices only** (matching the App Store / Google Play install path on the landing page), **Age 18+**, and kept Optimized Targeting (`audience_expansion`) off.
2. **Audience & Intent Refinement**:
   - Follower lookalikes (`LI5`) generated only 1 link click across 2,850 impressions and were paused on Day 1.
   - Removing the broad top-level *Business & Finance* category and focusing on the specific *Business & Finance / Entrepreneurship* interest paired with high-intent sole trader keywords (`sole trader`, `sole traders`, `self employed`, `freelancer`, `freelancing`, `invoicing`, `small business`, `business debit card`, `business account`, `self assessment`) improved link click rates while ensuring clicks came from sole traders.
   - Splitting **Ireland (`LI6`)** and the **UK (`LI1`)** into dedicated non-overlapping ad groups eliminated internal auction competition and allowed Ireland to scale alongside the larger UK market.
3. **Creative Selection**:
   - **Post `2142411163038965262`** (*"Sole trader? Wrenwell is a business debit card plus invoices sent from your phone..."*) delivered the strongest sustained link-click volume (175 of the 182 total link clicks) while pre-qualifying users on every detail on the landing page (sole trader eligibility, invoicing + automatic payment matching, 3 months free then €7/£6 per month, IE & UK, iPhone & Android).
   - **Post `2142773550907671340`** (*"€0 for three months..."*) underperformed on link-click rate (only 3 link clicks across 9,796 combined impressions on `LI2` and `LI4`) and was paused on Day 2.
   - **Post `2142592356972799672`** (*"Be honest: how many of your invoices are still sitting in drafts?"*) drew mostly non-click engagements (25 engagements to 1 link click on Day 2) without qualifying users on the paid debit card offer, so budget was consolidated into Post `2142411163038965262`.
