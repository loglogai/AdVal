### Yuzustride Halloween 2027 Campaign Recap

The 9-day Halloween campaign (`536eg`) ran across the full flight from **Saturday, October 23, 2027 (00:00 EDT)** through **Sunday, October 31, 2027 (23:59 EDT)**, strictly targeting **iOS and Android** users in the **United States and Canada** (`audience_expansion: DEFINED` to prevent ineligible desktop or out-of-region traffic).

#### Overall Campaign Results
* **Total Spend:** **$694.80** *(694,804,308 micro USD out of the $700.00 budget)*
* **Total Impressions:** **615,284**
* **Total Link Clicks:** **316** *(Average CPC: $2.20)*
* **Total Engagements:** **4,325**

#### Key Flight Optimizations
1. **Strict Eligibility Filtering:** All ad groups enforced `US` + `Canada` and `iOS` + `Android` targeting with `DEFINED` audience expansion so budget was only spent on devices and regions capable of starting a free trial.
2. **Reallocating to Top Performers:** Early monitoring showed **LI2 (Anime & Streaming Interests)** delivering the highest link-click volume and lowest CPC ($1.96), while **LI4 (Follower Lookalikes)** lagged at $3.00/click. LI4 was scaled down and paused after Day 4, and daily/total budgets were progressively shifted into **LI2** and **LI3** to maximize qualified traffic through the October 31 deadline.
3. **Creative Qualification:** Post `2244664939118766301` (which explicitly states device and country eligibility in the copy) naturally earned 68.4% of link clicks and pre-qualified users before they landed on `https://yuzustride.com/halloween`.
