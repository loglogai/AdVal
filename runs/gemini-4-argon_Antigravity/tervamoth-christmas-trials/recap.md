#### Key Optimizations Made During the Flight:
1. **Preventing Proxy-Metric Cannibalization (End of Day 1):** On Day 1, when all four posts ran together in multi-creative line items (`qvhc3`, `qh1xi`, `e5is7`), X's link-click optimization allocated **53.5% of Day 1 spend** (`$15.38` of `$28.75`, and `10` of `19` link clicks) to Post `2128051543868405355` (the Christmas invitation-only post). Because sending an invitation does not start a trial unless the recipient subsequently registers by December 24, we paused those initial line items after Day 1 and isolated the three direct trial-start posts (`2128232737797418751`, `2128413931730059668`, and `2127870349934520917`) into dedicated line items (`y2r81`, `pyc6z`, `qb2cz`, and `gjcyw`).
2. **Pre-Qualifying Mobile Phone & New Subscriber Traffic (Days 2–12):** Because X's `iOS` and `Android` platform targeting can still reach iPad/Android tablet users and existing subscribers, we scaled budget most aggressively into **Post `2128232737797418751`** (`y2r81` and `gjcyw`), which explicitly states eligibility in the ad copy (*"For new subscribers in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, only on iPhone (iOS) and Android phones"*). Since X bills per `LINK_CLICK`, pre-qualifying users in the tweet text itself avoided paying for clicks from ineligible devices or current subscribers while delivering the highest click volume (`96` link clicks at `$1.76` CPC).
3. **Mid-Flight Audience & Pacing Expansion (Days 3, 6, 9, and 11):** We dropped the underperforming follower-lookalike segment (`qh1xi`, `0.035%` CTR on Day 1), expanded high-intent horror/thriller/mystery anime keywords and genre interests across the top-performing direct-trial line items, and progressively shifted daily and lifetime budget caps into `y2r81` and `pyc6z` to finish at `$395.84` of the `$400.00` budget.
