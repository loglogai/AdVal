### Flight Management & Optimization Recap

1. **Day 1 (Jul 16 PDT) — Multi-Segment Launch:** Launched four US 18+ ad groups testing Broad National (`x0xab`), Civic & Policy Interests (`onot0`), Urban & Public Access Keywords (`e26c0`), and Errands/Family/Travel Interests (`4xbep`) with both approved posts. Broad National (`x0xab`) immediately led in both CTR (`0.0409%`) and CPC (`$2.50`), while Post `2208471451039109695` (*"Summer errands need a restroom stop..."*) outperformed Post `2208652644972278028` by **50% in link clicks** (`30` vs. `20`).
2. **Day 2 (Jul 17 PDT) — Shifting to High-CTR Broad + Top Creative:** Paused the lowest-performing interest segment (`4xbep`, `0.0227%` CTR / `$4.49` CPC), scaled `x0xab`, and launched a dedicated US 18+ Broad National ad group (`fxsre`) focused exclusively on the winning creative (`2208471451039109695`).
3. **Days 3–5 (Jul 18–20 PDT) — Scaling Winners & Pacing to Budget:** As `x0xab` and `fxsre` continued to deliver the highest click volume and strongest CTR (`0.0360%` overall on `fxsre`), we progressively shifted budget away from the narrower interest/keyword ad groups (`onot0` paused on Day 4; `e26c0` paused on Day 5) and calibrated final-day budgets so the flight finished cleanly at **$983.40** spent and **237 link clicks**.
