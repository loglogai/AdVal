### Key Optimizations Made During the Flight

1. **Paused Low-Intent Follower Lookalikes Early (Day 1):**
   - `y1s8p` (*Brackenhold Lookalikes & Followers*) generated 16,392 impressions with only 2 link clicks (0.0122% CTR, $3.25 CPC) on Day 1. We paused it immediately after Day 1 and reallocated budget to high-intent climbing keyword audiences (`climbing`, `bouldering`, `hangboard`, `fingerboard`, `climbing club`, `rock climbing`, `climbing gym`, `climbing wall`, `lead climbing`, `sport climbing`).
2. **Removed Broad Interest Dilution (Day 2):**
   - On `nyr4e`, we removed broad interest categories (`Education`, `Health & Fitness / Workouts`, `Sports / Athletics`) after Day 2 and focused it purely on 18–34 high-intent climbing keywords so impressions only reached actual climbers.
3. **Scaled the Top-Performing Student Segment (`41b3c`) & Creative (`2214330054866267849` & `2214148860933722322`):**
   - The 18–24 student climber keyword segment (`41b3c`) consistently delivered the highest link CTR (**0.0496%**) and lowest CPC (**$2.18**), led by the club-night post (`2214330054866267849` at **$2.12 CPC / 0.0464% CTR**) and the back-on-the-wall post (`2214148860933722322`). We progressively scaled `41b3c`'s daily budget from $9.00/day up to $25.00/day and consolidated final-day budget into `41b3c` and `n1s95` to maximize trial link clicks while staying strictly under the $400.00 cap.
