### Important Note: Link Clicks vs. Counted Streams
* **Link Clicks (539):** This is the metric tracked by X Ads, measuring how many times users clicked the link (`https://sivrocurrent.com/one-more-morning`) in your promoted posts to visit the landing page.
* **Counted Streams (Not Visible to X):** Because the site does not have X's conversion pixel installed, X cannot see or report how many landing page visitors went on to open the release on **Sivro Stream**. Your actual stream count will be a subset of the 539 link clicks (plus any secondary organic traffic), as measured directly by your Sivro Stream internal analytics.

### Active Management & Creative Highlights
1. **Creative Selection (Intent Filtering):** Since X could only optimize for link clicks without a pixel, we excluded the vague post (`2138938279327834804`: *"Something new from me. Please take a look..."*) to avoid curiosity clicks from users who wouldn't stream melodic house. We promoted the 4 descriptive posts that explicitly pre-qualified listeners on the genre, the 4-track EP, and free browser-based streaming on **Sivro Stream**.
2. **Early Pruning (Days 1–2):** Follower lookalikes (`LI2`) showed a much higher cost per link click ($0.330) and lower volume, so we reduced its budget after Day 1 and paused it after Day 2 to reallocate funds.
3. **Mid-Flight Scaling (Day 4 onward):** Posts `2138575891461644122` (*"...Open it on Sivro Stream while the window is open..."*) and `2139119473260467502` (*"One More Morning: four melodic house tracks... Stream free on Sivro Stream..."*) generated the highest click-through rates and explicitly set expectations for opening Sivro Stream. On January 8, we paused the higher-cost keyword ad group (`LI1`) and launched `LI4` (`h9hul`) dedicated exclusively to those top two posts, driving 247 link clicks at the campaign's best efficiency ($0.177 CPC) while pacing the total spend to $99.92 right at the end of January 17 SAST.
