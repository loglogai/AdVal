### In-Flight Adjustments Made
1. **Day 1 Pruning of Low-Intent Lifestyle Interests:** Midday on July 11, the keyword ad group (`kzxtd`) was serving impressions primarily on broad lifestyle interests (*Home & Garden* and *Family*) without generating link clicks. Removing those two broad interests so the ad group focused strictly on strength and home-exercise keywords immediately turned `kzxtd` into the strongest driver of link clicks for the rest of the flight.
2. **Budget Reallocation Toward High-Intent Search/Keyword Traffic:** By Day 3, the keyword-targeted ad group (`kzxtd`) was delivering both the highest click volume and the strongest link CTR (0.090%), with Post `2206282024354198610` explicitly calling out *"12-minute home sessions designed for life after 60"* driving the highest click-through rate. We shifted the majority of the remaining budget ($310 cap, $90/day) into `kzxtd` while trimming the lower-CTR broad ad group (`mqvwx`) and higher-CPC general health interest group (`jpf4n`).
