### Key Optimizations Made During the Flight
1. **Strict Eligibility Filtering:** Locked every ad group to **United States** and **iOS** with `audience_expansion=DEFINED` so zero budget was wasted on non-iPhone or non-US users.
2. **Audience Pruning:** On Day 1, broad Sports/Nutrition interest targeting (`qth43`) yielded a 0.025% CTR ($3.29 CPC), and on Day 2 broad home-gym keywords (`is4w1`) yielded a 0.046% CTR ($2.16 CPC). Both were paused early, and budget was reallocated to `vx0ra` and `oua2b` (`Workouts` + `Health & Fitness` paired with `dumbbell workout`, `home workout`, and `strength training`), which delivered 99% of our clicks at $1.78–$1.89 CPC.
3. **Creative Message-Match:** Concentrated 99% of spend (`$442.12` / `235` of `237` link clicks) on the three high-intent posts (`2213877070035552849`, `2213514682171477349`, and `2214058263967969601`) that matched the `/trial` landing page offer.
