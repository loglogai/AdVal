### Performance by Approved Post & Key Optimizations

1. **Initial Testing (Days 1–3)**:
   - We launched `LI1`–`LI4` testing all three approved posts across Travel & Forex Intent, Travel Interest, Broad iOS, and Broad Android.
   - **Post `2135873081967601291`** (*"New Year's resolution: sort travel money before packing..."*) and **Post `2136054275898471957`** (*"Is 'actually take that trip' on your New Year's resolution list again?..."*) drove **65 link clicks and 11 installs** across the first 3 days, whereas **Post `2136235469833064499`** (*"January 12, 2027 is the cutoff..."*) spent $20.05 for only **9 link clicks and 1 install** ($20.05 CPA).
2. **Focusing on Winning Posts (Days 4–14)**:
   - At the end of Day 3, we paused `LI1`–`LI4` and launched `LI5` (`8hn8b`), `LI6` (`b5hyc`), and `LI7` (`omf9w`) promoting **only Post `2135873081967601291` and Post `2136054275898471957`**, eliminating wasted spend on the underperforming cutoff-focused creative.
   - Across the full 14-day flight:
     - **Post `2135873081967601291`** delivered **182 link clicks** and **26 app installs** on $160.34 spend (**$0.88 CPC / $6.17 CPA**).
     - **Post `2136054275898471957`** delivered **149 link clicks** and **25 app installs** on $167.80 spend (**$1.13 CPC / $6.71 CPA**).
     - **Post `2136235469833064499`** delivered **9 link clicks** and **1 app install** on $20.05 spend before being rotated out.
3. **Dynamic Budget Reallocation & Pacing**:
   - Throughout Days 4–14, we rebalanced daily budgets every 3 days between `LI5` (Travel & Forex Intent), `LI6` (Travel Interest), and `LI7` (Broad Mobile) to prevent audience saturation in the narrower interest/keyword pools while capturing low-CPA installs in the final 48 hours before the January 12 fee-waiver cutoff.
   - All line items and the parent campaign have been paused following completion of the flight on January 12, 2027.
