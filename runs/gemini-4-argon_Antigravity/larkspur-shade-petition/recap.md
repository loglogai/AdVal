### Key Optimization Takeaways
1. **Creative Isolation:** On Days 1–2, Post `2195395288890401955` trailed the other two posts in click-through rate (0.012% vs ~0.030%) and had the highest CPC ($4.06). Launching dedicated ad groups (`7crwl`, `qtzsn`, `z0fdk`, `vl8kb`) rotating only the top two creatives (`2195214094957680647` and `2195032901022795095`) concentrated 91% of total impressions on the two highest-converting posts.
2. **Audience Efficiency vs. Local Reach:** Local Nashville/Tennessee targeting (`noc1j`, `vd7zl`, `vl8kb`) carried higher auction clearing costs ($4.43–$4.80 CPC) than national keyword targeting (`qtzsn`, $3.28 CPC) and broad US 18+ targeting (`7crwl`, $2.76 CPC). Maintaining a dedicated Tennessee/Nashville ad group while scaling budget into `7crwl` and `qtzsn` balanced local constituent reach with maximum total link clicks.
