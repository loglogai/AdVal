### Flight Adjustments Summary
- **Launch:** Split the $450.00 USD budget equally ($150.00 total / $30.00 daily per ad group) across AG1 (Nature & Birdsong Discovery), AG2 (Valentine's & Couples), and AG3 (Broad iOS) to test audience efficiency with both posts.
- **Mid-Flight Reallocations (Days 2–4):** AG1 and AG3 consistently delivered stronger click volume and lower cost per link click ($0.56–$0.61) compared to AG2 ($0.71–$0.87). Notably, the Valentine's Two Together post (`2152542923781691605`) resonated especially well when shown to the Nature & Birdsong audience in AG1. We progressively shifted daily and total budget caps from AG2 into AG1 and AG3 while keeping the combined line-item total budget capped at exactly $450.00 USD.
- **Final Day Pacing (Day 5):** Switched delivery to accelerated on the final afternoon so the remaining budget would be utilized before the Valentine's Day midnight deadline in Auckland, finishing at $449.16 USD spent.
