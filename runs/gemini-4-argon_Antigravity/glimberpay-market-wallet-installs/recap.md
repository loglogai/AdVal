### What Worked & How We Optimised

1. **Strict Device & Location Discipline:** Because Glimberpay is available only in the UK on iPhone (iOS) and Android phones, all ad groups were strictly constrained to **United Kingdom (`GB`)** and **iOS + Android** platforms (`WEBSITE_CONVERSIONS` goal tied directly to the `"Wallet installed"` event tag `uxtnr`). This prevented wasted spend on desktop or tablet users who cannot install the app.
2. **Playful, Relatable Problem Framing Won Decisively:** Post `2202371255301263080` (*"Bought the bread. Found the cheese. Lost the receipt somewhere near the tomatoes?"*) was the standout performer. In our initial 3-day test across LI1 and LI2, it generated nearly 3x the link-click rate of the more feature-heavy posts and drove the lowest cost per install. By mid-flight, we paused the initial broad-rotation ad groups (LI1 & LI2) and concentrated budget into LI3 and LI4 around `2202371255301263080` (alongside the secondary performer `2202733643170090864`, which led with *"£0 to download. No monthly wallet fee"*). Overall, `2202371255301263080` delivered **20 of the 28 total installs (71%)** at a **$12.37 CPI**.
3. **Combining Market Keywords with Food & Shopping Interests:** While food/shopping interests (`Shopping`, `Food`, `Cooking`, `Baking`) provided scale in LI1, the high-intent market keywords (`farmers market`, `market stall`, `food market`) and `Personal Finance` interest in LI2 produced a higher click-through rate and lower cost per install ($14.85 vs. $20.73 in the first 6 days). Combining both audience signals in LI3 and LI4 allowed X's conversion optimization to scale volume while lowering CPI to **$11.57** across the second half of the flight.
4. **Timely Final-Day Urgency Push:** We held Post `2202914837102979029` (*"9 July is the last day to install Glimberpay..."*) until 9 July (BST) so the copy was factually accurate on the deadline day. Introducing it for the final 24 hours produced the highest click-through rate of the campaign (**0.14% CTR**, 14 link clicks on 10,143 impressions) and captured **2 completed installs** for just **$9.96** ($4.98 CPI).
