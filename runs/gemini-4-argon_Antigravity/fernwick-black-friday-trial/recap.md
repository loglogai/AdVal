#### Key Takeaways & Optimizations Made During the Week
1. **Removing Desktop-Only Constraints:** On Day 1, the desktop-only test group (`wfiro`) delivered zero link clicks due to restricted inventory. On Day 2, we removed the platform filter and shifted budget into the two stronger ad groups (`vjd3w` and `bxj0m`).
2. **Audience Expansion & Pacing:** Mid-week, we enabled Optimized Targeting (`EXPANDED` audience expansion) and scaled budgets on the **Software Dev & Gaming Interests** and **Game Engine Keywords** ad groups, which drove steady link clicks at ~$1.49–$1.74 CPC while keeping total spend safely capped under $500.00.
3. **Top-Performing Messaging:** Post `2123461297568149236` (which explicitly called out Unity, Unreal, Godot, the 60-day Pro trial, and US/Canada eligibility) was the standout winner, generating **50.8% of all link clicks (156 clicks)**, followed by the urgency-focused Cyber Monday deadline post (`2123642491501123261`) with **87 link clicks**.
