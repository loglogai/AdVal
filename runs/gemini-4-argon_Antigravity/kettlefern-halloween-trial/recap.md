### Key Management Decisions Made During the Flight

1. **Pre-Qualifying for Completed Free-Trial Starts (No X Pixel):**
   - Because X could only optimize for `LINK_CLICKS` (`WEBSITE_CLICKS` objective) and could not see trial completions on `kettlefern.app/lantern-evenings`, we strictly enforced landing-page eligibility (`LOCATION`: **United Kingdom & Ireland**, `AGE`: **`AGE_OVER_18`**, `audience_expansion`: **`DEFINED`**) on every ad group.
   - We excluded Posts `2243321084115164635` and `2243502278046994173` from spend because neither mentioned anime, streaming, the 7-day free trial, or the £4.99/month subscription, which would have attracted unqualified curiosity clicks with high landing-page bounce rates. Instead, we focused 100% of spend on Posts `2243139890184686641` and `2243683471980145632`, which explicitly pre-qualified users on both genre (`slice-of-life and iyashikei anime`) and pricing (`7-day free trial, then £4.99/month`).
2. **Active Optimization Across Ad Groups & Flight Days:**
   - **Days 1–3:** Tested all 4 qualified ad groups (`AG1`–`AG4`). `AG1` (`82grf`, Anime Interest + Post `6641`) and `AG4` (`wrlbs`, Iyashikei & Slice-of-Life Keywords) delivered the lowest CPCs (`$2.38` and `$2.42`) and highest CTRs (`0.0704%` and `0.0483%`), while `AG3` (`mhixx`, Lookalikes) lagged at `$3.24` CPC and `0.0292%` CTR. We paused `AG3` after Day 3.
   - **Days 4–7:** Scaled `AG1` and `AG4`, paused `AG2` (`eat1r`) after its CPC rose to `$3.65`, and moderated mid-week budgets on Wednesday/Thursday when weekday CPCs were higher so we could reserve extra budget for Halloween weekend.
   - **Days 8–10 (Halloween Weekend):** Scaled daily caps on `AG1` and `AG4` into Friday Oct 29 – Sunday Oct 31 as weekend/Halloween engagement surged (`22` link clicks at `$2.41` CPC on Friday, `23` link clicks at `$2.43` CPC on Saturday, and `22` link clicks on Halloween Sunday), finishing at **$497.93** total spend without exceeding the $500.00 cap.
