### Setup & Optimization Strategy
* **Objective & Optimization:** Configured for `WEBSITE_CLICKS` with a `LINK_CLICKS` optimization goal (paying per link click) since the X pixel is not installed on `tembusumama.app/newyear`.
* **Core Guardrails:** Every ad group strictly targeted **iOS (`PLATFORM: 0`)**, **Female (`FEMALE`)**, **Ages 18–49 (`AGE_18_TO_49`)**, and **excluded Russia (`RU`) and Belarus (`BY`)** worldwide.
* **Daily Management:** We launched three distinct targeting ad groups with all three approved posts and monitored performance daily. After Day 1 and Day 3, we shifted budget toward **AG3 (Broad + Optimized Targeting)**, which consistently achieved the lowest cost per link click ($0.51) and highest click volume. All ad groups and the campaign are now paused and completed.
