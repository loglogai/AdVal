### Key Optimizations Made During the Flight

1. **Eligibility & Compliance Filtering**: Restricted all targeting strictly to the **United States and Canada** (matching your financial services licensing and X pre-authorization) and **iOS and Android** devices (excluding desktop and non-supported devices that cannot install from the app stores on `https://tervilo.app/school`).
2. **Eliminated Low-Intent Creative & Broad Targeting Early (Day 1)**:
   - Paused `q25x5` (Post `2212246324639150169`) after 8 hours on Day 1 once it recorded 1,179 impressions with 0 link clicks (and lacked qualifying copy for money transfer/school support).
   - Paused `z0x5j` (untargeted broad mobile) on Day 1 to concentrate budget on high-intent remittance and back-to-school audiences more likely to convert to app installs after landing on the page.
3. **Shifted Budget to Keyword Targeting & Urgency Creative (Days 2–5)**:
   - High-intent keyword targeting (`3basu` and `swrnx`) consistently delivered higher CTRs and lower CPCs ($1.62–$1.64) than interest-only targeting ($1.76–$2.05).
   - As the July 28 deadline approached, Post `2212427518575471207` (*"July 28 is the cutoff..."*) saw its Link CTR climb from 0.039% on Day 1 to >0.064% on Days 3–5, so daily budget was progressively shifted toward `swrnx`.
4. **Bid Optimization**: Calibrating `MAX` bids down from $3.00–$3.50 to $2.40–$2.50 reduced average CPC from **$1.90** on Day 1 to **~$1.52–$1.55** on Days 4–5 while maintaining full daily delivery.
