### 4. Key Observations & What Worked

1. **Practical "How-It-Works" + Explicit Pre-Qualification (`2150625287996295112`) Outperformed on Both Click Rate and Install Intent:**
   - On Day 1, when all three posts ran head-to-head inside the same ad groups, Post `2150625287996295112` achieved more than **2x the link CTR** of Post `2150262900128148936` (`0.163%` vs. `0.059%` in Relationships & Couples; `0.107%` vs. `0.045%` in Photography; `0.080%` vs. `0.047%` in Broad iOS) and also beat the Valentine Keepsake Pack post (`2150444094061922123`).
   - Because Post `2150625287996295112` leads with concrete steps (*"Choose three photos, add dates and captions, then save one collage image"*) and states key eligibility terms up front (*"free to download with six everyday layouts included. iPhone (iOS) only; available worldwide except China"*), users who clicked through to `https://havlumi.app/valentine` were already pre-qualified on price, device, and functionality—maximizing downstream conversion from link clicks to completed iPhone installs.
2. **Isolating the Winning Post Cut Cost per Link Click by 38%:**
   - Pausing the mixed-creative test ad groups after Day 1 and isolating Post `2150625287996295112` in `LI5`, `LI6`, and `LI7` reduced overall campaign cost per link click from **$0.548 on Day 1** down to **$0.338–$0.493** over the rest of the flight, lifting daily link clicks from **136 on Day 1** to **200+ per day** during the peak pre-Valentine window.
3. **Audience Trade-Offs (High-Intent Relationships vs. Broad iOS):**
   - **Relationships, Dating, Marriage & Family + Valentine Keywords (`LI5`)** generated the highest engagement intent (**0.1146% link CTR**), reaching users actively thinking about a partner or family keepsake ahead of February 14.
   - **Broad iOS ex-China (`LI6`)** paired with the self-qualifying copy of Post `2150625287996295112` delivered the lowest cost per link click (**$0.338 CPC**, 579 link clicks) while maintaining a strong **0.0901% link CTR**.
   - **Photography, Design & `@Havlumi` Follower Lookalikes (`LI7`)** provided a balanced middle ground (**0.0959% link CTR** at **$0.450 CPC**, 223 link clicks).
