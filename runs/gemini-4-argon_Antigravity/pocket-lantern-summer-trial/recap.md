### Key Optimizations Made During the Flight
1. **Strict Eligibility & Intent Filtering:** Restricted all delivery to **UK & Ireland**, **iOS & Android** platforms, and adult age brackets (`21+` / `25+` parent & decision-maker demographics) to ensure clicks came from users able to start a free trial on supported devices.
2. **Creative Allocation:** Post `2220128260716165837` ("cancel before it ends and pay nothing") consistently achieved a ~50% higher link click-through rate and lower CPC than Post `2219947066782760857`, so we launched a dedicated `SITE_VISITS` line item (`LI4`) promoting the winning post exclusively.
3. **Budget Rebalancing:** Shifted daily budget midway through the flight toward `LI3` (Follower Lookalikes & High-Intent Keywords, which delivered the lowest CPC at $1.93) and `LI4` (`SITE_VISITS` optimization), while scaling down `LI1` and `LI2`. All campaigns and ad groups are now paused at flight completion.
