### Key Optimizations Made During the 9 Days
1. **Geo-Gating & Objective Alignment**: Since the site had no X conversion pixel and streaming was restricted to listeners in Ireland and the UK, all line items were restricted to **Ireland + UK** and set to `WEBSITE_CLICKS` (paying only per link click) to maximize qualified landing page visits.
2. **Pruning Underperforming Audiences & Copy**:
   - **LI3 (`soumw`)** tested follower lookalikes combined with Horror/Thriller interests and included the generic Post `2244770635578862025`. After two days, it showed a substantially higher cost per link click ($2.91 CPC) and zero link clicks on the generic post, so LI3 was paused at the end of Day 2.
3. **Scaling High-Intent Keyword & Site-Visit Line Items**:
   - **LI2 (`bhmll`)** (targeting Samhain, Halloween, choir, and Irish folk music keywords) and **LI4 (`75f75`)** (optimizing specifically for `SITE_VISITS` across music/streaming and folklore audiences) delivered the highest click volume and lowest CPCs ($1.68 and $1.72). Daily budgets were progressively shifted from LI1 and LI3 into LI2 and LI4 through Halloween night.
   - Posts explicitly highlighting **40 Cork voices, old Irish ghost songs, and free streaming in Ireland & the UK** (`2244227053780022236` and `2244589441645788254`) drove the strongest click volume and efficiency.
