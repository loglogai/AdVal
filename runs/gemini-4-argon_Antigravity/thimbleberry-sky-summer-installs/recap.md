### Key Creative & Targeting Takeaways

1. **Top-Converting Posts**:
   - **Post `2207021899578845232`** (*"$0. That's what it costs to learn the name of every star above your campsite..."*) was the #1 driver of installs, generating **15 installs** across the run (including 8 installs in `46r9u` at **$13.07/install** and 3 installs in `1t63v` at **$4.91/install**).
   - **Post `2206659511713467882`** (*"Point your iPhone at the sky and Thimbleberry Sky tells you what you're looking at..."*) was the #2 performer, generating **8 installs** across the run.
   - **Post `2207203093512577924`** (*"Thimbleberry Sky names stars, planets & constellations live... iPhone (iOS) only, for stargazers in the US & Canada."*) generated the highest click-through rate on `r2wp1` (67 link clicks) and **4 installs**.
   - **Post `2207384287441979603`** (*"Perseids season is coming..."*) generated **3 installs** early in the run, while **Post `2206840705645306260`** (*"That super bright 'star' over the trees last night?..."*) generated curiosity clicks without a single install (0 installs) and was rotated out early.
2. **Audience & Bidding Efficiency**:
   - Constraining every ad group strictly to **iOS (`PLATFORM=0`)** and **United States + Canada (`LOCATION`)** while letting X's conversion optimization (`goal=WEBSITE_CONVERSIONS` on pixel `4i4bb`) find converters broadly achieved a ~$0.94–$1.16 CPM, outperforming narrower interest and keyword layers that carried a 35–55% CPM premium.
