### 2. What the Reporting Actually Shows (Link Clicks vs. Trial Starts)

- **No Conversion/Trial-Start Visibility in X Ads:** Because `https://jovunarapilates.app/start` does not have an X conversion pixel (`web_event_tag`) or mobile app conversion tracking installed, **X Ads reporting only measures upper-funnel delivery and engagement metrics (`impressions`, `link_clicks`, `engagements`, and `billed_charge_local_micro`)**. It cannot observe or report how many of those **1,082 link clicks** completed a landing-page visit or started a 7-day free trial in the App Store.
- **How We Optimized for Trial-Start Eligibility:** Since X could only optimize for `LINK_CLICKS` (`WEBSITE_CLICKS` objective), we enforced strict eligibility rules on every ad group so budget was never wasted on clicks that could not convert into trial starts:
  1. **Exclusively iOS (`PLATFORM = iOS`):** Excluded Android, desktop, and other devices so every click came from an iOS device capable of installing the iPhone-only app.
  2. **Worldwide Except China (`LOCATION NE China`):** Excluded China (`CN`) across all ad groups while leaving all other markets open to leverage your localized landing page and app.
  3. **No Unapproved Copy:** Used only your three approved posts (`2138696687416686580`, `2138877881350341546`, `2139059075284323251`).

**Key Adjustments Made During the Flight:**
- **Cut Underperforming Lookalikes Early (`LI4`):** After 6 hours, `LI4` (Followers & Lookalikes) showed a **$1.35 CPC**—more than double the cost per click of our other three ad groups. We reduced its budget immediately and paused it at hour 18 (`$17.44` spend, `13` link clicks).
- **Scaled Health & Fitness Workouts (`LI2`) and High-Intent Pilates/Back-Care Keywords (`LI1`):** `LI2` consistently delivered the lowest CPC (**$0.55**) and highest link CTR (**0.115%**), followed closely by `LI1` (**$0.59 CPC**) and `LI3` (**$0.61 CPC**). Shifting budget into `LI2` and `LI1` across Days 2 and 3 reduced overall campaign CPC from **$0.75** in the first 6 hours to **$0.59** by the end of the flight.
