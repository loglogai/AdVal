1. **Filtering Out Unqualified Link Clicks After Day 1**:
   - Without an X pixel, X's `LINK_CLICKS` optimization naturally favors whichever creative and audience generate the cheapest raw clicks. On Day 1, Post `2227270321569957229` (*"Does your September reset need a reset? ... One less blank page"*)—which omits any mention of *warehouse*, *safety*, *Aisle Rehearsal*, or *20-minute*—and the `Education` interest in `c3tas` absorbed over a third of all clicks while starving Post `2227089127635860770`.
   - To prevent budget waste on general classroom/back-to-school browsers who bounce once they see the warehouse landing page, we paused the Day 1 ad groups after 24 hours, removed the `Education` interest and `EXPANDED` targeting, and shifted budget into ad groups (`ijv5q`, `qumh7`, `cngld`, `4as8p`) promoting posts that explicitly pre-qualify warehouse employers and supervisors (`2226726739770895417`, `2226545545835828797`, and `2227089127635860770`).
