We’re Glimberpay, a digital wallet business based in Leeds. We built the app around a familiar market-day muddle: buying something at several different stalls and then trying to remember which payment went with which purchase. At participating stalls, shoppers scan the trader’s QR code, check the payment details and pay from their topped-up wallet. Their payments stay together in the app, with the stall name attached, so the shopping trip is easier to make sense of afterwards.

Our customers are people who enjoy shopping at markets, whether that means a regular grocery trip or an occasional browse through independent stalls. We want the product to feel practical and approachable. Please keep our voice plain, lightly playful and specific about what the wallet does. Participation matters: we serve the United Kingdom, but we don’t claim that every market trader accepts Glimberpay. Our Leeds base is part of our story, rather than a limit on where customers can use the service.
