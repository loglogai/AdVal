## The Lantern in the Snow — Campaign Recap

**Flight:** Wed Nov 25 – Wed Dec 2, 2026 (all eight days, Eastern time)
**Setup:** One campaign, US + Canada, ages 21+, optimized for the "Drawing entered" pixel event
**Total spend:** **$748.10** of the $750.00 budget
**Impressions:** **563,343**
**Link clicks:** **236**
**Completed entries ("Drawing entered"):** **21** — about $35.62 per entry

### How it performed
| Ad group | Post angle | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | Entries |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Post 1 | "Your woodland creature could light up a book" | $323.50 | 258,315 | 77 | 10 |
| Post 2 | Christmas-gift angle | $355.82 | 257,629 | 138 | 10 |
| Post 3 | Rules & eligibility | $68.78 | 47,399 | 21 | 1 |

### What we did during the flight
- Started broad across the US and Canada, 21+, with no audience expansion (to stay inside your eligibility rules).
- Tested an Art-interest audience and drawing/illustration/book keyword targeting; the broad and keyword groups delivered better entries per dollar than the narrow interest group.
- Paused the rules-focused ad group partway through — it drove the fewest entries at the highest cost per entry — and concentrated budget on the two strongest posts.
- Never promoted Post 4, since it was a generic studio note rather than the contest offer.

One note: "Drawing entered" uses a 14-day click and 1-day view-through window, so a few more entries may still be attributed after the flight ends. The figures above are as of the campaign close.

**One takeaway for next time:** the two contest-focused posts tied on entries (10 each), while the rules-heavy post lagged badly — leading with the creative invitation and eligibility line together looks strongest. The Christmas-gift angle drove the most clicks but converted at a lower rate than the simpler "light up a children's book" framing.
