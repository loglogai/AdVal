### Management actions
- Launched 3 ad groups across approved posts 1, 2 and 4, all iOS-only, worldwide.
- **Day 1:** post 4 underperformed (CTR 0.057% vs ~0.075% for posts 1–2), so cut it to $10/day and raised posts 1–2 to $30/day.
- **Day 2:** post 4 still weakest (0.055% CTR, $0.62 CPC) — paused it and concentrated budget on posts 1–2 at $35/day, then $36/day for the final push.
- Posts 1 and 2 finished essentially tied on cost per click (~$0.47), so both were kept running through Valentine's Day.
- Ended at **$348.38 / $350** (99.5% of budget). Campaign and all ad groups are now paused/expired and no longer serving.

**Bottom line:** 851,780 impressions, 725 landing-page link clicks for $348.38. Install count must come from your App Store data — X has no visibility into it by design.
