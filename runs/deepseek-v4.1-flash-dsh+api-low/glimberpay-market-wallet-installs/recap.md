## Glimberpay market-wallet campaign — final recap

**Flight:** Sunday 27 June – Friday 9 July 2027 (UK time)
**Objective:** app installs, counted via the X pixel event “Wallet installed”

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Spend | **$384.82** of the $400 budget |
| Impressions | **353,450** |
| Link clicks | **206** |
| Installs (“Wallet installed”) | **27** |
| Cost per install | **~$14.25** |

**What worked**

- **The straightforward QR/feature post did the heavy lifting.** “Pay by QR at participating UK market stalls…” (post 2202552449234154346) produced 15 of the 27 installs. Clear, specific copy — QR payment, free download, no monthly wallet fee, free receipt sorting, iPhone/Android — gave people enough to act on.
- **The deadline post earned its budget late.** “9 July is the last day…” (post 2202914837102979029) converted efficiently in the final stretch, when the offer deadline became a real reason to install. It finished with 9 installs from about $95.
- **The playful “lost receipt” post was a modest third.** It pulled clicks but converted fewer of them (3 installs); it stayed as a small test arm rather than the main spend.
- **The “£0 to download” post was the weak link.** It absorbed early impressions with almost no click-through, so I paused it on day two and moved its budget to the performing posts.

Targeting stayed tight to the brief throughout: United Kingdom only, iPhone (iOS) and Android phones only, with the “Wallet installed” pixel as the optimisation event. As the data came in I shifted budget toward the QR explainer, then toward the deadline message for the final days.

One honest caveat: 27 installs is a small base, so treat the per-post differences as directional rather than definitive. The pattern was consistent, though — plain, specific copy about how the wallet works beat the purely punny line.
