**What I did and learned**
- Objective was Website Clicks optimized for link clicks, US-only, since there's no pixel — that makes clicks to `eldergulch.com/season3` the best on-platform stand-in for streams. Actual streams you'll confirm from Spotify/Apple.
- The broad "mine shaft breathe" hook won early, so I cut the expensive Horror-interest group on Day 1.
- Midweek I added a **keyword-intent** ad group (people posting/searching "horror podcast," "audio drama," "podcast," "true crime podcast," etc.). It became the runaway winner at **$1.28/click** — better than half the cost of interest targeting — so I shifted the back half of the budget to it.
- Daily budget was re-tuned throughout; final spend landed $9.36 under the cap because delivery tapered on the last night.

**One honest caveat:** 419 link clicks is traffic to the landing page, not confirmed streams — some visitors won't press play. If you want to gauge true cost-per-stream, compare your platform download/stream counts for Oct 24–Nov 1 against this traffic. For next season, adding X's pixel (or a UTM-tagged redirect you can reconcile) would let us optimize on the real thing instead of a proxy.
