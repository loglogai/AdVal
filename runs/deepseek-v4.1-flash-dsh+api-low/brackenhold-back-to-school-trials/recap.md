## Brackenhold Back-to-School 2027 — Final Recap

**Flight dates:** Fri 30 Jul – Tue 10 Aug 2027 (Dublin time)

### What ran
- One campaign, targeting Ireland + United Kingdom, iPhone and Android only.
- Three ad groups: broad reach, climbing-keyword intent, and a Health & Fitness interest test.
- All four approved posts were used; no new posts were created.

### Best-performing creative

Second: **"Term starts in a few weeks…"** (2214330054866267849) with 72 clicks.

### Notes for next time
- Broad targeting was cheapest per click and delivered the most volume; the Health & Fitness interest group was the weakest and was retired mid-flight.
- Because there's no X pixel, I optimised for link clicks. **Trial starts won't appear in X's reporting** — you'll need to match them on your side, ideally with a UTM-tagged landing URL or by comparing your trial-start numbers to the click volume above.
- Text-only promoted posts kept CTR modest; adding creative/video to the post (rather than changing copy) is probably the biggest lever for a future push.
