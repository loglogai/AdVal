Hello from a slightly chalky office in Cork! We're Brackenhold, a small team of climbers who got fed up scribbling hangboard sessions on the backs of gym receipts, so we built an app for it. It gives you a week-by-week training plan (fingerboard, bouldering, ropes), times your hangs and logs every session so you can see yourself getting stronger.
