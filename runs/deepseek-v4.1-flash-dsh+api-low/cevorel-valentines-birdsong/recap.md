### On the outcome you care about
These figures are **campaign activity only** — impressions and link clicks on X. X has no pixel on your site, so it cannot see or report installs. Your own completed first-time install count from the App Store is the real outcome and will need to be read from your own analytics/App Store data, not from this campaign. The 704 link clicks are the hand-off point: people who tapped through to the landing page, from which installs happen.

One note for next time: link-click rate was modest on broad worldwide targeting, which is expected for a quiet, curiosity-led product rather than a hard-sell app ad. If you run this again, a small test against bird/nature-interest targeting could be worth trying alongside the broad audience.
