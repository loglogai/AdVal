### How it played out
- Launched with all four posts in separate ad groups ($27.50/day each), targeted to US + iOS/Android platforms only.
- **Post 3 was the clear loser** — cheap impressions but almost no clicks ($3.24/click) — so I paused it after day one and recycled its budget.
- **Post 4 became the workhorse** — best click rate and lowest cost per click — and I steadily shifted budget to it. By the end it carried ~52% of spend and 58% of clicks.
- Post 1 held second; Post 2 was fine but never beat the other two, so I folded it in on day three.
- I deliberately held budget in reserve to make sure we were live and spending on Halloween itself, then opened the taps on the final day. That worked: delivery accelerated sharply on Oct 31 and we landed at **$549.49 of $550**, with the campaign ending on schedule.

Two caveats worth flagging: iOS platform targeting is the finest grain X offers, so iPads can't be separated out from iPhones — desktop/web were fully excluded. And since trial starts aren't visible to X, the click numbers above are the best proxy; your own trial-start data is the real scoreboard.
