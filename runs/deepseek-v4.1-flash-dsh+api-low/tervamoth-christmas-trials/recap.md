## Tervamoth Christmas campaign — final recap

**Flight:** Monday 7 December – Friday 18 December 2026 (Nairobi time), 12 days — completed.

**X-reported results (delivery metrics only):**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Spend | **$390.66** of the $400.00 budget (97.7%) |
| Impressions | **459,566** |

| Blended cost per link click | **$1.76** |

**How it was set up:** one campaign, four ad groups — one per approved post — targeting Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, on iPhone (iOS) and Android only. Because the site has no X pixel, X cannot see trial starts, so the campaign was optimised for website/link clicks and we treated clicks as directional, not as the counted result.

**What changed during the flight:**
- Day 1 used anime/horror–thriller interest targeting. A broad-targeting test on one ad group produced clicks at roughly half the cost, so all groups were switched to broad geo + mobile targeting from day 2.
- The “7 days free / no purchase” creative was the weakest and was paused on day 10 after three consistent days at the highest cost per click.
- Budget was progressively concentrated on the strongest creative, the details post (“Stream horror and thriller anime… new subscribers in Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa, only on iPhone and Android”): **104 of the 222 link clicks at ~$1.63 each**.
