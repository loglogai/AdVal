### What I learned
- **Post D (the "how many pull-ups?" question) was the winner** — most clicks and cheapest per click; B was a close second. They carried most of the budget from day 2 onward.
- **Post E underperformed badly** (0.029% CTR) and was paused after day 1.
- **Interest targeting vs. broad:** the Health & Fitness / Workouts-targeted copy of D got ~2x the click-through rate, but with ~2.5x the CPM, so cost-per-click was worse. I kept it as a small hedge rather than scaling it.
- Delivery was steady at ~$130–155/day and we landed just under budget.
