**What ran**
- One campaign, UK + Ireland, iOS + Android only, optimised for link clicks (there's no X pixel, so X can't see trial starts — link clicks are the closest proxy).
- Post B (the "hand-picked, age-rated anime… cancel before it ends and pay nothing" copy) carried nearly all delivery.
- Started with two creative/targeting tests; Post A underperformed and was paused on 22 Aug, with budget shifted to Post B.
- Broad UK/IE mobile targeting beat the anime-interest targeting on cost per click, so it took the majority of the budget; a smaller anime-interest ad group stayed live alongside it to keep some qualified reach.
- Budget was paced across the flight and the final hours were opened up to use the remainder; total landed at 98.7% of the $350 cap.
