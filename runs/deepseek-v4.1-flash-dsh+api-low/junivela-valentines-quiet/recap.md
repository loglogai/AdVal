### How it was set up
- One campaign, three ad groups — one per approved post — optimized for **link clicks to junivela.app/valentine** (the closest proxy, since there's no app ID/pixel to optimize for installs).
- **iPhone/iOS only**, across a broad 13-market set spanning North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and Thailand (~125–144M reachable audience).
- Started at $20/day per post, then reallocated toward what worked.
