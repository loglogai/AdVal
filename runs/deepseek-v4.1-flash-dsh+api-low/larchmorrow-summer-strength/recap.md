### How it was set up
- One campaign, two ad groups, each running one of your two approved posts — no new copy created.
- Objective: website/link clicks, since trial starts happen in-app and can't be tracked by X.
- Targeting: United States + Canada only; iOS and Android phones only (no desktop/laptop, no other mobile web), matching the firm phone-only requirement.
- No age floor was applied — the over-60 focus is in the messaging, not a signup cutoff.
- Budget: $50/day per post for the first three days, then shifted to $45/$56 for the last two days toward the post that was earning cheaper clicks.

### What these numbers do and don't show
These are X-side delivery metrics only: impressions, clicks on the link, and spend. Because your site has no X pixel and trial starts are counted inside the app, **X cannot see or report how many 14-day trials were actually started**. Link clicks here are a proxy for landing-page traffic — not a trial-start count. To know the real outcome, compare these 237 link clicks against your own app reporting for trial activations during and shortly after July 11–15 (allowing for the usual delay between a click and an in-app signup).
