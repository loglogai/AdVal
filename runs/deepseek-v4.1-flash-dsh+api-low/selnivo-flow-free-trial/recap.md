## Selnivo Flow — campaign recap (Oct 20–27, 2027, Manila time)

**One campaign, website-clicks objective, mobile-only (iOS + Android), across 7 English-speaking markets.** Because the site has no X pixel, X cannot see trial starts — so I optimized for **link clicks to the trial page**, the closest trackable proxy. These are *not* verified trial starts.

### Results (all 8 days)

| Metric | Total |
|---|---|
| **Spend** | **$942.71** of $950 (99.2%) |
| **Impressions** | **878,523** |
| **Link clicks** | **491** |
| Avg. cost per link click | $1.92 |
| Link-click rate | ~0.06% |

### By post
| Post | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | CPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C — "chair" humor** | $718.03 | 690,949 | **403** | **$1.78** |
| A — "workday reminder" | $111.99 | 110,824 | 48 | $2.33 |
| B — "shoulder & hip mobility" | $112.68 | 76,750 | 40 | $2.82 |

### What happened
- All three posts performed nearly identically on Day 1, so I tested sharper audiences. **Interest targeting (Health & Fitness / Workouts) raised cost per click to $2.64–$3.49** and cut reach without adding efficiency — so I dropped it.
- The **chair-humor post** consistently won on cost per click and volume, so I paused A and B and concentrated spend on C, scaling it from $39 → $140/day as efficiency held.
- Final two days were the strongest (71 and 69 clicks/day) at ~$1.74–$1.85 per click.

### What the numbers actually show — and don't
- We generated **491 clicks to the trial page at a blended $1.92 each**. That is a traffic result, not a trial-start result.
- **Actual free-trial starts, and true cost per trial, are unknown.** Without the pixel, there is no attribution beyond the click. Conversion rate on the landing page is the missing piece — I'd recommend adding X's pixel (or a UTM/redirect-based tracker) before the next flight so we can optimize and report on trial starts directly.

Budget stayed within the $950 cap; campaign is now expired and paused.
