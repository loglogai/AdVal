### What I did
- Ran all four promoted posts as separate ad groups on a **link-click (website clicks)** objective, since the site has no X pixel — so X optimised for the landing-page click, which is the handoff point before someone opens a streaming service.
- Started all four evenly, then after 24 hours shifted budget toward the two best-performing posts (the "$0" and "pelican" creatives) and kept the Valentine's "send them a song" post running at a lower weight through the weekend.
- Delivered worldwide. Iran, North Korea, Cuba and Syria aren't offered as targetable countries on X Ads at all, so those audiences were excluded by default — no delivery there.
- The campaign ended on schedule Monday night; it is now marked expired and cannot spend further.

Since X can't see the streams themselves, the 508 link clicks are the number to compare against your streaming dashboards — that's visitors delivered to https://marrowlanescores.com.au/tidelines, from which they choose a service.
