| **Impressions** | **489,376** |

**By ad group**

| Ad group | Spend | Impressions | Link clicks | CPC | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broad — US & CA | $192.24 | 247,967 | 152 | $1.26 | 0.061% |
| Keywords — US & CA | $305.97 | 241,409 | 190 | $1.61 | 0.079% |

All four approved posts ran in both ad groups. Targeting was **US + Canada only** the entire flight (no other exclusions needed); bidding started AUTO, then moved to MAX caps after day one with two mid-flight adjustments as delivery shifted.

**What the numbers say**
- The keyword group (Unity/Unreal/Godot crash, crash reporting, error tracking, game crash) took 56% of clicks on 61% of spend and had the better CTR — consistent with the earlier read that intent-based targeting is where the quality is.
- The broad group delivered cheaper clicks ($1.26 vs $1.61) and reliably filled its budget, so I scaled it up later in the week. Black Friday was the strongest reach day (66k impressions), and the final day was the highest-spend day.
- Spend landed at $498.22, essentially the full $500.

**Important caveat:** There's no X pixel on the site, so X cannot see sign-ups — the 342 link clicks are the closest observable proxy for the primary result, not a sign-up count. I can't claim any conversion rate. To judge the real KPI, match these to your own trial/sign-up data for Nov 21–30; treat any "cost per sign-up" or sign-up volume as unverified until you do. The keyword group is the one I'd expect to convert best, but that's an inference from targeting, not measured.
