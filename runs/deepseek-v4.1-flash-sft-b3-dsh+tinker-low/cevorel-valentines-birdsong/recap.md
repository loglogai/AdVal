## Cevorel Two Together — X campaign recap

**What ran:** one campaign with three ad groups, all targeting iOS devices only, worldwide with Russia and Belarus excluded, all on the `ALL_ON_TWITTER` placement. Both approved posts were used. There is no X pixel and no app ID, so the campaign optimised for link clicks — X cannot see or report installs.

**Management notes:** bidding started on AUTO, moved to capped MAX bids after day one, was raised when delivery fell short on day two, and raised again for the final day. The keywords group carried the most budget and produced the most clicks; the broad group delivered the cheapest clicks; the interest group was the smallest and weakest performer, so it stayed on a small budget. The two posts performed similarly — the Valentine's post drew slightly more clicks in total, largely because it also ran in the interest group.
