## Wrenhollow Choir — Samhain Night Songs campaign recap

**Final settled results (23–31 October 2027, Dublin time)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Spend** | **$446.10** of the $450.00 budget |
| **Impressions** | **365,210** |
| **Link clicks** | **299** |
| Average cost per link click | $1.49 |

**By ad group**
- **Keywords — Irish folk / Halloween music:** $268.20 · 161,113 impressions · 181 link clicks ($1.48 CPC) — the clear winner.
- **Broad — all posts (IE + UK):** $119.49 · 157,074 impressions · 86 link clicks ($1.39 CPC) — most efficient per click.
- **Interest — Music:** $58.42 · 47,023 impressions · 32 link clicks ($1.83 CPC) — weakest, so it was scaled back.

All five approved posts ran; no new copy was created. Targeting was Ireland + United Kingdom only, with no age, device or platform limits added beyond what the brief specified.

**One honest caveat:** the site has no X pixel, so X cannot see or report streams — the 299 are link clicks to the landing page, an early proxy, not stream counts. Actual streams must be read from your streaming-service analytics. The campaign is now ended and paused.
