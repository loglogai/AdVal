### What this does and doesn't tell us
These are **link clicks, not trial starts**. Because the site has no X pixel, X cannot see a completed trial start, so it cannot report or optimise for that. **Trial starts must come from Tervamoth's own records**, and X's 312 clicks should not be treated as 312 trial starts — many clicks won't convert, and some conversions may land after the campaign ended. The same applies to invitations: an invitation sent is not a trial start.

### How it was set up and adjusted
- Targeting: Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana and South Africa; iPhone/iOS and Android phones only (no desktop, tablet or TV); no age limits added.
- Optimised for link clicks, the closest proxy available without a pixel; started on AUTO and moved to MAX bids once there was day-one cost data.
- Ran all four approved posts across the groups (no new posts created). Early on, the gift-invitation post ("for the friend who always guesses the twist") drew the strongest click-through.
- Kept the broad group at about a third of the daily budget throughout; after three days, paused the underperforming interest group and moved its budget into the keyword group. Later in the flight, raised budgets/bids to use the remaining budget as delivery opened up.
- Final spend $395.89, inside the $400 cap.
