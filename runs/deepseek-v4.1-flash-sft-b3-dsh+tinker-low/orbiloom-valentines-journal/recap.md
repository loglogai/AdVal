**Installs: not visible to X.** The site has no X pixel, so there is no pixel-based install or conversion number from this flight — everything above is X's own delivery data (impressions and link clicks to orbiloom.app/love). Installs happened, if they happened, on the App Store side and aren't in these figures. Please treat the link-click count as the closest proxy, not as installs.

**What ran** — one campaign, "Orbiloom Valentine 2027 — Installs (iOS, worldwide)", website-clicks objective optimized for link clicks (the only install-facing goal available without a pixel), X feed placements:

| Ad group | Targeting | Spend | Impr. | Link clicks | CPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broad | iOS only, worldwide | $158.74 | 516,723 | 360 | $0.44 |
| Keywords | iOS + 6 phrases (journaling app, journal app, gratitude journal, self care journal, valentines gift, valentines day gift) | $85.78 | 177,399 | 141 | $0.61 |
| Interest | iOS + Relationships interest | $95.26 | 160,869 | 164 | $0.58 |

**Management done during the flight**
- Started on AUTO bidding, moved all three groups to MAX caps after day 1 at 1.3–1.5× the observed cost per click.
- Delivery fell to ~$43/day on day 2, so I raised caps ~25–30%; pace recovered to full daily budget and held for the rest of the run.
- Day by day I moved budget toward the cheapest clicks (broad won), ending at $36–38 / $16 / $18 per day. All daily budgets were fully spent except a small gap on Feb 14, which is the $10.22 unspent.
- Best creative: post 2 ("Give someone a reason to notice…", 233 link clicks, $0.51 avg) and post 1 (186 clicks, $0.50). Post 4 was efficient but drew less volume (138 clicks, $0.51). Post 3 ("Please take a look at our app…", the vague one) was clearly weakest — only 31 clicks and X throttled its delivery.

**Targeting compliance:** iOS platform on every ad group; no age limits added (none required for this category). All posts and the landing page state the February 14, 2027 unlock deadline, and every post carries it. Iran, North Korea, Cuba and Syria aren't in X's targetable location catalogue at all (I searched each), so there was nothing to exclude — worldwide delivery already can't reach them. The campaign ended on schedule and is now paused/expired, so nothing more will spend.
