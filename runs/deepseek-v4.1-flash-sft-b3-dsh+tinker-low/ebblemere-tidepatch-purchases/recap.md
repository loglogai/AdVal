**What happened / decisions**
- Started on AUTO bidding; after Day 1 switched to MAX bids at ~1.4× the AUTO cost per click.
- Broad won on efficiency by a wide margin (cheapest clicks, ~45% of all clicks). Keyword groups cost ~$1.30–1.45/click but reached higher-intent searches.
- Raised bids ~25% after Day 2 to fix underspend, then lifted Day-4 budgets to deploy the remaining budget. Tracked spend daily throughout.
- Used $2,292.90 of the $2,400 authorized — about **$107 under budget**, with each ad group hard-capped so the total could not exceed $2,400.

**Important caveat**
X has **no purchase/conversion data** for this campaign (no pixel), so I can't report purchases or cost per purchase — those must be counted in your store records. Treat the click figures above as cost/delivery metrics, not as the purchase result. Since I can't see which ad group produced buyers, the budget split stayed roughly equal rather than being heavily concentrated on the cheapest clicks.
