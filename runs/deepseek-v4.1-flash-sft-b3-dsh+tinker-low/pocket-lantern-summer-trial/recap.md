**What happened, honestly**
- No X pixel on the site, so **X never saw a single trial start** — it can't report them or bid for them. I optimized for **link clicks**, which is the closest thing X can measure; those are clicks to the landing page, not trials.
- Because the trial is 14 days, even with tracking, many trials started from clicks late in the flight wouldn't have converted by 30 August. The click numbers are a proxy, not a trial count.
- Started on AUTO bidding, then moved to MAX bids after day one. Broad pulled the most volume and the dominant share of budget; I kept the keyword and interest groups running at a smaller share because X's interest proxy is known to be imperfect. The anime-interest group was consistently the most expensive per click, so it stayed on the smallest budget.

**Budget:** ended $21.73 under the $350 cap; all three ad groups were still pacing and paused automatically at the flight end time. No changes needed from your side.
