## Gatelark Streets — X campaign recap (24 Jun – 4 Jul 2027, BST)

The campaign has now **expired** as scheduled and is no longer serving.

**Final settled numbers (X-reported)**

| Metric | Total |
|---|---|
| Spend | **$398.19** of the $450 budget |
| Impressions | **363,391** |
| Link clicks | **223** |
| Completed petition signatures (pixel) | **28** |

Blended cost per completed signature ≈ **$14.22**; CPC ≈ $1.79; CPM ≈ $1.10. Budget used 88%; $51.81 left unspent because delivery ran below the daily cap on several days.

**How it was set up**
- One campaign, three ad groups, all UK-only and **18+** (`AGE_OVER_18`), conversion-optimised to the “Petition signed” pixel, AUTO bidding for the first two days then MAX caps.
- **Broad** — all four approved posts: $121.92 · 142,474 impressions · 96 clicks · 7 sign-ups (~$17.42 each)
- **Phrase keywords** — school streets, 20 mph, speed limits, road safety, safe routes to school, traffic calming, school run: $138.96 · 123,741 impressions · 63 clicks · 12 sign-ups (~$11.58 each)
- **Interest** — Education + Relationships/Parenting: $137.32 · 97,176 impressions · 64 clicks · 9 sign-ups (~$15.26 each)

**What happened**
- Convincing delivery was the main issue, not budget: several early days spent well under the $40.80/day plan, so I raised under-delivering ad groups’ bid caps in steps.
- I kept the budget split even because no ad group established a clear, durable CPA advantage. Keywords finished strongest on both volume and cost per signature.
- Only the four approved posts were used; no new creative was written.

**Caveats for your interpretation:** conversions are what the X pixel attributed (post-click plus the tag’s 1-day post-view window), so 28 is X’s attributed total, not necessarily every signature on the page. Daily volumes were small, so day-to-day swings in cost per signature are noisy. All targeting stayed within the brief — United Kingdom only and 18+ — consistent with X’s political-campaigning rules.
