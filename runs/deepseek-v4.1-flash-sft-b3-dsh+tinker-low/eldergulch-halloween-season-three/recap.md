### What this means — and what it doesn't
**X can't see a stream.** With no pixel on the site, the last thing X can measure is the click to `eldergulch.com/season3`. So the 476 link clicks are people who reached your landing page — the gateway hop before someone taps through to Spotify or Apple Podcasts. **The actual stream count has to come from your podcast platform dashboards.** Expect the stream number to be below 476 (some visitors won't listen, and the page links out to multiple apps), and treat X's click figure as a ceiling, not a stream count. X's platform doesn't report country-of-conversion or view-through beyond that either, so there's no hidden "likely stream" count I can give you honestly.
