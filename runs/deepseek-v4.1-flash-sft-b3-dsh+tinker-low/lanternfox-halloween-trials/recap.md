### What we did and learned
- **Structure:** 1 campaign, 3 ad groups — a broad reach group, a buyer-intent keyword group (dubbed anime, watch anime, anime streaming, ghost/yokai anime, brand name), and an anime-interest group — so reach didn't depend on one bet.
- **Budget:** started $110/day ($40 broad / $40 keywords / $30 interest). After two full days, broad was clearly the cheapest traffic and interest consistently the priciest, so we shifted budget toward broad/keywords, then nudged caps up on the final two days to use the budget. Everything landed under the $550 cap.
- **Creatives:** all four approved posts ran; no new posts written. The broad group carried all four, with posts 1 and 2 (ghost-story and "$0 for 14 days") generally the most efficient.
- **Honest caveats:** with no pixel, **X cannot report trial starts** — those live in your own analytics. The 272 link clicks are site arrivals, not confirmed trials, so treat CPC/CTR as directional, not conversion proof. Platform targeting is set to iOS + Android only (desktop, mobile web, other devices excluded), which is the finest device split X offers — there's no separate phone-vs-tablet switch, so a small share of tablet impressions within those families is possible.
