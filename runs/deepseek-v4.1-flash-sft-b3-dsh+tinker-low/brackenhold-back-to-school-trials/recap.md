- All four approved posts were used; no new copy was written.

### What we did
- Set up one campaign with three ad groups, each geo-targeted to Ireland **and** the UK only, restricted to **iOS and Android**, on timeline placements. No age or gender limits (not required for this category).
- **Broad** on ~a third of budget from day one; **phrase-keyword** group on buyer-intent terms (climbing training, fingerboard, hangboard, bouldering, etc.); started a general fitness-interest group as a test.
- Switched from AUTO to MAX bids after day one; paused the fitness-interest group on day 2 (1 click for $3.09) and reallocated to the two working groups.
- Added a second keyword group mid-flight ("bouldering", "climbing gym", "climbing club", etc.) to reach incremental buyers, and scaled up the two best groups over the final two days.
