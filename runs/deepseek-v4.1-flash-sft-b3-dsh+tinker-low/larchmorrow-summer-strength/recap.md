### What these numbers do and don't tell you
- These are **impressions and link clicks only** — they show how many people saw the ads and clicked through to larchmorrow.com/start.
- **X cannot see trial starts.** There is no X pixel on the site and the 14-day trial is activated inside the app, so X has no way to attribute a landing-page visit or app download to an actual trial start. **Any link click is not evidence of a trial start.**
- The figures that matter for your outcome — **free-trial starts and the cost per trial start** — have to come from your own app reporting, joined to this flight's timing (July 11–15). I can't compute a cost per trial start, because X never observed one.
- Because there was no pixel, the campaign was optimized for link clicks, not conversions. The relative ranking above (broad cheapest per click) is a proxy for volume, not proof of which audience produced more trial starts — a keyword-triggered click and a broadly-targeted click may convert very differently. Your app data is the only way to settle that.
