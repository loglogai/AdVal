### What ran
- **Worldwide** delivery. No age limit, no platform restriction, all X placements.
- Iran, North Korea, Cuba and Syria were excluded as required. Worth knowing: X’s ad platform doesn’t offer those four countries as targetable locations at all, so they were never reachable by the campaign.
- Keywords used: film score, movie soundtrack, instrumental music, piano music, cello music, wedding songs, love songs, study music. Interests: Music, Film Festivals & Award Shows.
- Bidding: AUTO on day 1 to learn the price, then MAX. The first MAX cap was too tight and throttled delivery, so I raised it; by days 3–5 all groups were spending at pace.
- Creative: the **“$0 — free to stream”** post was the clear winner — 281 of the 570 clicks. The launch post took 176, the closing-credits post 70, and “send them a song” 43.

### The honest caveat
Your site has no X pixel, so X cannot see a stream, a play, or a streaming-service click. Everything above stops at **link clicks to your landing page**. Whether those 570 clicks turned into 570 streams — or 200, or 40 — is only visible in your streaming dashboards, and that’s the number you should judge this on. The $0.34 average cost per link click is the one efficiency figure I can stand behind.

One note for next time: about $7.86 of the budget went unspent because the keyword group ran out of searchable volume and the last day’s totals capped out. Allocating that to the broad group earlier would likely have bought another ~25 clicks.
