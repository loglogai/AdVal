### What ran
- **1 campaign**, 2 ad groups, all budgets on the ad groups.
- **Markets:** Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa only.
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android phones only.
- **Objective:** website conversions, optimized to your verified **"App installed"** pixel tag — **not** an app-install campaign, and no app ID was set, since installs happen from the app-store links on the landing page.
- **Creatives:** only your 3 approved posts; **no new posts written**.
- **Bidding:** started AUTO, moved to MAX caps and adjusted through the flight.

### What worked
- **Keyword intent was the strongest lever:** the "Travel money & FX" ad group (6 buyer-intent phrases) delivered **55 of 78 installs**; the broad group delivered **23**.
- Broad prospecting converted in bursts and then went cold for a couple of days, so I shifted budget toward keywords in the final stretch. That rebalance is why delivery finished at ~$348 rather than leaving more budget unspent.

### Honest caveats
- **Installs are X-attributed**, not first-party verified: the pixel uses a 14-day post-click / 1-day post-view window. They are **app installs, not completed exchanges** — which matches what you said you count.
- Spend came in **$2.05 under** the $350 total; the daily caps plus auction delivery left a small remainder on the last day.
- **Financial-services policy:** your account's pre-approval covered NG/GH/ZA. X's policy data returned **no minimum age** for financial services, so I applied no age restriction rather than inventing one (the brief mentioned an age rule — flagging that so there's no surprise).
- The campaign is now **EXPIRED** and not serving; no further spend will accrue.

If it were run again, I'd consolidate further behind the keyword-intent group and keep only a small broad test for reach.
