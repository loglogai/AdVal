These are X’s reported figures using the account’s 14-day click and 1-day view attribution windows. Pixel numbers can settle slightly after a flight and won’t always match app-store or analytics totals exactly.

**What worked**
- **Keyword intent was the engine.** Market- and payment-intent keywords (“farmers market”, “market stalls”, “pay by QR”, “digital wallet”) produced 25 of the 29 installs. Broad and interest targeting produced the other 4 and were more expensive.
- **The strongest creative was post 2202190061369539840** — “Market payments, gathered in one wallet… no monthly wallet fee… 30 days of free receipt sorting.” It drove roughly 17 installs. The 9 July deadline post (2202914837102979029) was the cheapest per install, with 7, and the QR/iPhone-Android post (2202552449234154346) added 5. The bread/cheese story post and the £0 download post recorded no installs.
- **We tuned toward keywords:** widened the keyword list, concentrated budget there, added a dedicated group running the winning post, and paused the interest group after it spent about $26 for one install.

**Worth knowing**
- Daily results were lumpy — several zero-install days followed by catch-up — so day-by-day cost per install was noisy. The total is a small sample and best treated as directional rather than a precise benchmark.
- Targeting stayed on brief throughout: UK only, iPhone (iOS) and Android phones only, no desktop or tablet, and no extra demographic limits added (the financial-services policy didn’t require an age floor). All five approved posts were used; no new ones were written.
