## Campaign recap

The campaign ran Jan 6–8, 2027, Dhaka time, targeting iOS users worldwide except China.

- **Spend:** $644.43 of the $650 budget
- **Impressions:** 1,123,531
- **Link clicks:** 1,002

X’s reporting showed impressions, spend, and link clicks. It did **not** show trial starts because the site doesn’t have X’s pixel, so these results don’t tell us how many clicks became free trials. The three posts had similar click costs during monitoring, so I kept them running. The campaign and ad group are now paused.
